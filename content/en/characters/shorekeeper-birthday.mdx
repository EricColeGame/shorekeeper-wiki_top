## Why the Shorekeeper Birthday Matters More Than You'd Think

Shorekeeper is one of Wuthering Waves' most talked-about Resonators, and every year a wave of players goes hunting for one very specific detail: the Shorekeeper birthday recorded in her character profile. It looks like a throwaway line of text, but in a game where every playable character has a canonical date of birth, that single entry becomes the anchor for fan art, tribute videos, and coordinated community events. Getting the Shorekeeper birthday right matters because it's the one piece of character trivia that fans can actually celebrate together on a fixed date.

This guide walks through where the date lives in-game, how to verify it without relying on secondhand sources, why players so often confuse it with her banner release, and how the community marks the occasion.

## Release Date, Banner Date, and Birthday: Sorting Out the Timeline

This is where most confusion starts. Players searching for a Shorekeeper birthday often land on her banner date instead, because that's the date that gets plastered across news posts and pull videos.

| Date type | What it refers to | How reliable is it? |
|---|---|---|
| Banner release date | The day her Convene banner went live | Very reliable — widely documented |
| Banner end date | When her first banner closed | Reliable |
| Rerun dates | Later banners she appeared on | Reliable but varies by region |
| Birthday | Her canonical date of birth in the profile | Reliable only if read in-game |
| "Anniversary" | One year after her release | A fan construct, not official |

The key takeaway: a character's release date and her birthday are two completely separate things. Shorekeeper's banner going live in a particular month has nothing to do with the date printed in her profile. If you've seen a post confidently stating that her birthday is the same day her banner launched, that's almost certainly a mix-up.

There's also a subtler trap. Because she debuted partway through the game's lifespan, some players assume her birthday must fall in the same month as her release. There's no rule in Wuthering Waves that ties the two together — Resonators' birthdays are scattered across the calendar regardless of when they were added to the roster.

## Common Mistakes and Misinformation

A few patterns come up again and again when players go looking for this date:

- **Trusting a search snippet over the game.** Search results sometimes surface a release date and label it as a birthday.
- **Assuming every wiki agrees.** When two fan sources conflict, the in-game profile wins every time.
- **Expecting free pulls.** Character birthdays in Wuthering Waves have not historically come with dedicated login rewards, so don't plan around Astrites you probably won't get.
- **Mixing up characters.** Several Black Shores characters have profiles that look similar at a glance, which makes misattribution easy.
- **Forgetting time zones.** If you're coordinating a community post, agree on a single time zone up front or you'll split the celebration across two days.

None of these are serious problems, but they're the reason the same question gets asked every single year.

## FAQ

**When is Shorekeeper's birthday?**

The canonical date is the one printed in her Basic Info panel inside the in-game Archive. Because that panel is maintained by the developers, it's the only version worth treating as definitive. Fan wikis and social posts are useful shortcuts but should be treated as community reports, since dates get copied, edited, and mixed up with banner launches over time.

**Is the Shorekeeper birthday the same as her release date?**

No. Her release date is when her Convene banner went live, which is a matter of public record and easy to confirm. Her birthday is a separate piece of character lore stored in her profile. Confusing the two is the single most common error players make when searching for this information.

**Does Wuthering Waves give rewards on character birthdays?**

As of writing, Kuro Games has not built recurring in-game birthday events around individual Resonators, so you shouldn't expect a special login bonus or a birthday banner. The celebration is community-driven — fan art, screenshots, tribute videos, and Discord threads rather than free pulls.

**How can I join the celebration if I don't own Shorekeeper?**

You can still take part. Share fan art, comment on tribute posts, or save up and pull for her on a rerun so you're ready for next year. Many community threads are open to anyone interested in the character, owner or not — the point is the appreciation, not the roster.

**Where should I double-check if I find a conflicting date?**

Open the game and read her profile directly. That takes about two minutes and settles the question permanently, which is a much better use of time than comparing three wikis that disagree with each other.
