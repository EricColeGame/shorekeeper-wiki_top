Shorekeeper is one of the most flexible supports in Wuthering Waves, and a properly tuned Shorekeeper build guide can turn her from a comfortable healer into the biggest damage multiplier on your account. If your main DPS feels like it has plateaued, the fix is rarely a new weapon or a luckier echo roll — it is usually a support who hands out team-wide crit stats while keeping everyone alive. That is exactly what she does. This Shorekeeper build guide walks through stat priorities, echo sets, weapon options, team comps, and the small rotation habits that separate an average Shorekeeper from a great one.

## Why Shorekeeper Deserves a Permanent Slot

Shorekeeper is a 5-star Spectro Resonator who wields a Rectifier. Her kit is built around a persistent field — often called the Stellarealm in community discussions — that follows the active resonator and hands out offensive buffs while a heal-over-time effect keeps the team topped up. Because the field travels with whoever is on the field, she does not force you into a specific position or punish you for swapping characters mid-fight.

What makes her special is the crit component. Community reports consistently describe her as the first support in the game to push team-wide Crit Rate and Crit DMG, which is why she shows up in nearly every endgame clear.

| Attribute | Shorekeeper at a Glance |
|---|---|
| Rarity | 5-star |
| Element | Spectro |
| Weapon type | Rectifier |
| Role | Healer and team buffer |
| Primary scaling | HP for healing, Energy Regen for her crit buff |
| Field time required | Very low |
| Works at base copies | Yes — her core value is not locked behind duplicates |

The practical takeaway: she is not a "nice to have" healer. She is an offensive amplifier who happens to heal, and that framing should drive every decision in your Shorekeeper build guide.

## Shorekeeper Build Guide: Stat Priorities and Benchmarks

Most players over-invest in healing and under-invest in Energy Regen. Healing has diminishing returns once your team stops dying; Energy Regen keeps scaling into damage because it feeds her crit buff and her Liberation uptime.

| Priority | Stat | Why It Matters | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Regen | Drives her team-wide crit bonus and keeps her field available | Push this far higher than you would on a typical DPS |
| 2 | HP% | Her healing scales with max HP | Take it on every echo main stat you can justify |
| 3 | Flat HP | Same scaling, smaller contribution | A perfectly fine filler substat |
| 4 | Healing Bonus | Improves raw healing only, not buffs | Useful while your roster is still under-geared |
| Avoid | Crit Rate / Crit DMG | She deals negligible personal damage | Do not chase crit on her |
| Avoid | ATK% | Does not scale her healing | A wasted substat |

Player experience suggests the sweet spot for most accounts is Energy Regen in the 230–250% range, with the exact cap shifting as the game is patched. Check the in-game tooltip on her Liberation before you commit, because that number is the single biggest lever in any Shorekeeper build.

### Common Stat Mistakes

- Stacking crit because "crit is good on everyone." It is not good on her.
- Ignoring Energy Regen because the healing already feels sufficient.
- Running an ATK% 4-cost echo out of habit from other supports.
- Over-investing in Healing Bonus when the team is already surviving comfortably.

## Echoes, Sonata Sets, and Weapons

This is the part of a Shorekeeper build guide that generates the most arguments, because two very different philosophies both work.

### Best Sonata Sets

| Sonata Set | What It Provides | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Rejuvenating Glow (5-piece) | Healing boost plus a team ATK buff after healing | The default choice for most players |
| Moonlit Clouds (5-piece) | Energy Regen plus an ATK buff after her Outro Skill | Teams where she outra on a tight, frequent loop |
| 2-piece + 2-piece HP% mix | Raw HP with no set bonus requirement | Interim gear while you farm the real set |
| Celestial Light | Spectro DMG bonus | Not recommended — she is not a damage dealer |

If you are unsure, start with Rejuvenating Glow. It is forgiving, it reinforces her role, and it does not require perfect rotation discipline to pay off.

### Echo Main Stats by Slot

| Echo Slot | Recommended Main Stat | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| 4-cost | HP% | Healing Bonus if your team is fragile |

| 3-cost | HP% | Energy Regen if you are under your ER target |

For the 4-cost slot, support-oriented echoes that grant team-wide Energy Regen or ATK effects are popular in community builds because they stack with her own buffs. Prioritize whichever one your roster already has at a high level.

### Weapon Options

| Weapon | Rarity | Why You Would Use It |
|---|---|---|
| Stellar Symphony | 5-star | Her signature Rectifier; HP and Energy Regen synergy plus a team buff. Best in slot if you have it |
| Variation | 4-star | Energy Regen substat and extra Concerto energy generation — the classic budget pick |

The good news is that weapon choice is the least punishing part of a Shorekeeper build. She functions well on a 4-star Rectifier, so do not pull for her signature unless you are already committed to the character.

## Team Comps and Rotations That Work

Shorekeeper fits into almost any team because her buffs are not element-locked. The only real question is which damage dealer benefits most from a crit window.

| Team Archetype | Main DPS | Sub-DPS / Amplifier | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spectro hypercarry | Jinhsi | Zhezhi or Sanhua | Her crit field lines up beautifully with Jinhsi's burst windows |
| Havoc basic-attack | Camellya | Sanhua | Shorekeeper covers healing so the sub-DPS can run pure damage |
| Aero heavy-attack | Jiyan | Mortefi | Long field windows mean the Stellarealm stays active through the whole burst |
| Electro skill-focused | Xiangli Yao | Yinlin | Her buffs apply to skill damage just as well as basic attacks |
| Fusion bruiser | Changli | Any off-field sub-DPS | Works, though Changli benefits slightly less from a crit-focused buff |

### A Simple Rotation to Copy

| Step | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Swap to Shorekeeper and use her Resonance Skill | Starts healing and begins her kit loop |
| 2 | Basic attacks to build Concerto energy | You need full Concerto to trigger her Outro |
| 3 | Cast her Resonance Liberation | Places the buff field that follows the active resonator |
| 4 | Swap out with her Outro Skill into your main DPS | Passes the buff and gives the DPS a clean field |
| 5 | Run your DPS window, then repeat | Re-enter Shorekeeper only when her field or heals need refreshing |

Two habits matter more than perfect execution. First, do not leave her on the field longer than necessary — her value comes from buffs, not from her own damage. Second, reapply her Liberation before your biggest damage window, not after, so the crit bonus covers the burst rather than trailing behind it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Shorekeeper worth building if I already have another healer?**
Yes. She is less of a healer and more of a team-wide damage amplifier, so she usually replaces a pure healer rather than competing with one. This Shorekeeper build guide treats HP and healing as a baseline, with the crit buff as the real payoff.

**What Energy Regen should I aim for in a Shorekeeper build?**
Community reports point to roughly 230–250% as a comfortable target, but verify the current cap on her Liberation tooltip. Exceeding the cap wastes substats you could spend on HP.

**Can Shorekeeper work as a main DPS?**
Not realistically. Her multipliers are tuned for support, and her kit rewards field time spent elsewhere. Building her for damage means giving up the buffs that make her worth a slot.

**Does she need her signature weapon to be good?**
No. A 4-star Rectifier with Energy Regen or HP% covers her needs, and the difference is far smaller than the difference between good and bad echo rolls. If you are following this Shorekeeper build guide on a budget, put your resources into echoes first and weapons last.
