## Why Shorekeeper Is the Easiest Premium Support to Build for Free

Shorekeeper is one of the few five-star supports in Wuthering Waves who reaches near-peak performance without duplicates or a signature weapon. That is exactly why a **Shorekeeper F2P build** is so approachable: her value comes from Energy Regen, HP, and healing output rather than crit stats, so gear you already farmed for your healers often works as-is.

Most damage dealers live or die by their weapon banner. Shorekeeper does not. Her team-wide buffs are tied to how much Energy Regen she can stack and how reliably she can deploy her field, both of which are reachable with four-star and even three-star gear. If you pulled her as a free-to-play player and felt overwhelmed by build guides full of premium assumptions, this **Shorekeeper F2P build** guide is written for you.

Below you will find stat targets, weapon rankings, echo setups, team options that use free characters, and a rotation you can practice in a single session.

The key takeaway: a **Shorekeeper F2P build** is a support build first and a stat-stick build second. Chasing crit damage on her is the single most common waste of resources.

**Variation** is the community-favorite pick, and for good reason. Its Energy Regen substat feeds directly into the stat that powers Shorekeeper's buffs, and its passive supports smoother rotations. If you have never leveled it, this is the moment.

If you do not own Variation yet, do not dismantle your four-star rectifiers. Any of them will carry you through the story and most endgame content. Player experience consistently shows that a well-rolled support weapon beats a poorly invested premium one, because Shorekeeper's contribution is measured in uptime, not damage numbers.

## Echo Build: Sets, Main Stats, and Substat Priority

Echoes are where a **Shorekeeper F2P build** actually comes together. You want two things: a healing-oriented set for the team buff, and as much Energy Regen as the substat system will give you.

### Recommended Echo Sets

| Set | Pieces | When to Use It |
|---|---|---|
| Healing/support set (Rejuvenating Glow) | 5 | Default choice for almost every team |
| Energy Regen support set (Moonlit Clouds) | 5 | Only if another healer already runs the healing set |

|---|---|---|
| Energy Regen echo (Fallacy of No Return) | Team attack buff + Energy Regen | Hypercarry teams that want more damage |

Community reports suggest aiming for roughly 250% total Energy Regen to cap her crit-rate and crit-damage buffs. Treat that as a target, not a wall — you will still feel strong at lower numbers while you farm better pieces. Because her buffs scale off Energy Regen rather than raw stats, a single well-rolled Energy Regen substat can be worth more than an entire premium weapon.

Community reports also note that her buffs apply to the whole team, which means every point of Energy Regen you farm benefits all three characters at once — an efficiency that few supports can match.

## Rotation and Gameplay Tips

A **Shorekeeper F2P build** only performs if you actually use her kit in the right order. Her on-field time should be short; the damage dealer should own the rest of the fight.

| Step | Action | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intro Skill into the field | Opens with healing and starts building energy |
| 2 | Resonance Skill | Begins her support state |
| 3 | A few basic attacks or Forte actions | Charges her field and Concerto |
| 4 | Resonance Liberation | Deploys the buff field for the team |
| 5 | Outro Skill into your DPS | Passes the team buff and clears the field |
| 6 | Repeat every cycle | Keeps buff uptime near permanent |

Practical tips that free-to-play players often miss:

- **Do not linger on field.** Every second Shorekeeper spends attacking is a second your DPS is not. Her job is to hand off quickly.
- **Watch the field timer.** Deploy her ultimate, then swap immediately so the buff window overlaps your damage window.
- **Use her healing proactively.** Triggering heals before a big hit keeps your team topped up and keeps the support set bonus active.
- **Practice the swap timing in the training area.** A clean two-second rotation is worth more than a perfect echo roll.
- **Upgrade her Forte and Liberation first.** These two nodes carry nearly all of her value; basic attack nodes can wait.

## Common F2P Mistakes to Avoid

Even players following a good **Shorekeeper F2P build** stumble on the same handful of traps.

- **Building her like a damage dealer.** Crit weapons and crit echoes look impressive but contribute almost nothing to her role.
- **Ignoring Energy Regen substats.** This is the one stat that changes how strong your team feels.
- **Running two healers.** Shorekeeper already covers healing; a second healer usually costs you damage.
- **Hoarding upgrade materials for her weapon instead of her Forte.** Her kit matters more than her weapon refinement.
- **Skipping the shield or energy main echo.** The right main echo often outperforms a higher-rarity stat stick.
- **Swapping out too late.** Her buffs are on a timer, and a late swap wastes the entire cycle.

## FAQ

**Can I run a Shorekeeper F2P build without her signature weapon?**
Yes. A four-star rectifier with an Energy Regen substat covers the vast majority of her value. The signature weapon is a luxury upgrade, not a requirement, and most free-to-play accounts clear endgame content without it.

**How much Energy Regen should a Shorekeeper F2P build aim for?**
Community reports point to roughly 250% total Energy Regen as the point where her crit buffs max out. Start by stacking Energy Regen on your weapon and 3-cost echoes, then add substats over time.

**Is Shorekeeper worth building if I already have another healer?**
Usually yes, because she is a buffer as much as a healer. If your current healer only sustains the team, Shorekeeper adds a damage multiplier on top — which is why she is a common pick in free-to-play teams.

**Which echoes should I farm first for this build?**
Start with a five-piece healing or support set and a 4-cost Healing Bonus echo. Once your Energy Regen is comfortable, refine the substats and consider an Energy Regen or shield main echo depending on the fight.
