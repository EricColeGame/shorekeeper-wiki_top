Shorekeeper is the kind of support that quietly decides fights. She keeps your team standing, feeds them Crit stats, and slots into almost any composition in Wuthering Waves without forcing you to rebuild your entire roster. That flexibility is exactly why a **Shorekeeper quick guide** is worth ten minutes of your time before you start burning Echoes, Tunes, and upgrade materials.

## What Shorekeeper Actually Does in a Team

Shorekeeper is a Spectro support who wields a Rectifier. She isn't a damage dealer, and judging her by her own numbers misses the point. Her value is uptime: she heals, she amplifies, and she makes your main damage dealer look better on the scoreboard.

Lore-wise, she's tied to the Black Shores and to the Rover's story, but mechanically she's the most flexible sustain unit in the game — she doesn't care what element your carry is.

Her kit does three jobs at once:

- **Sustain** — continuous healing that keeps chip damage from snowballing into a wipe.
- **Amplification** — a team-wide Crit buff that makes every damage dealer hit harder.
- **Flexibility** — no elemental requirement, so she fits into teams built around any main DPS.

| Part of her kit | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Resonance Skill | Applies healing and starts building her Forte resource | Prevents small mistakes from becoming deaths |
| Forte Circuit | Converts her resource into an enhanced state | Determines how strong and how long her buff window is |
| Resonance Liberation (Ultimate) | Creates a healing zone that also boosts team Crit stats | Her signature contribution to a fight |
| Outro Skill | Passes a buff to the next resonator you swap to | Rewards correct swap order |
| Inherent Skills | Passive healing and Energy Regen bonuses | Free value that costs you no rotation time |

## Shorekeeper Quick Guide: Build Priorities at a Glance

Here's the short version: **Energy Regen is the build.** Everything else is secondary. Community testing suggests her Crit buff scales with her Energy Regen past a threshold, which is why players chase a high Energy Regen number before they chase anything else.

### Stat Priorities

| Priority | Stat | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Regen | The single most important stat. Players commonly target the community-recommended breakpoint rather than stopping halfway |
| 2 | HP% | Improves her survivability and her healing output |
| 3 | Healing Bonus | A strong 4-cost Echo main stat if you want bigger heals |
| 4 | Flat HP / DEF | Filler stats that keep her alive in harder content |
| Low | Crit Rate / Crit DMG | She isn't a damage dealer — don't chase these |

### Weapon Options

| Tier | Weapon type | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Best | Her signature 5-star Rectifier | Highest Energy Regen plus a team-wide damage buff |
| Strong | Other 5-star Rectifiers with Energy Regen | High base stats and ER, just without the signature passive |
| Solid | Variation (4-star Rectifier) | Energy Regen substat and a support passive — the standard budget pick |
| Acceptable | Any Rectifier with Energy Regen or HP% | Better than leaving the slot empty while you farm |

### Echo Sets and Main Echo

| Slot | Recommendation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 5-piece set | Rejuvenating Glow | The default healing-focused support set |
| Main Echo | Fallacy of No Return | Grants a team ATK buff when its skill is used |
| Alternative main Echo | A defensive or HP-scaling Echo | Take this if you're dying more than you're buffing |
| Substats | Energy Regen first, then HP% | Reroll anything that rolls pure offense |

Use this **Shorekeeper quick guide** build order literally: Energy Regen to the breakpoint, then HP%, then Healing Bonus, then whatever survivability you can find. Resist the urge to build her like a sub-DPS.

## Best Team Compositions for Shorekeeper

Shorekeeper's job is to make one strong damage dealer stronger while keeping everyone alive. Because she has no elemental requirement, the team-building question isn't "who does she pair with?" but "who is my best carry?"

| Team shell | Main DPS | Flex / Sub-DPS | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard hypercarry | Your best-built on-field DPS | A sub-DPS who buffs or debuffs | Shorekeeper covers all sustain; the other two focus purely on damage |
| Double support | A strong but fragile DPS | A second buffer or healer | Overkill on healing, excellent for the hardest content |
| Quickswap | A fast-swapping DPS | A coordinated attacker | Her Outro buff rewards frequent swaps |
| Beginner-friendly | Any DPS you own | Any sub-DPS | Works even with a half-built Shorekeeper |

A few practical notes from player experience:

- If your carry is squishy, double support is not a meme — it's a legitimate way to clear content you'd otherwise fail.
- Coordinated attackers benefit heavily from her buff window, since their damage keeps ticking while you're off-field.
- Don't overthink elements. Shorekeeper doesn't care, and neither should your team slots.

As with any **Shorekeeper quick guide**, the team advice here assumes she's built for Energy Regen. A damage-built Shorekeeper won't hold up her end of these compositions.

## Rotation Basics: How to Play Shorekeeper Smoothly

Shorekeeper is a swap-and-forget support, which means your rotation matters more than your reflexes. The core loop is short:

| Step | Action | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open with her Resonance Skill | Starts healing and begins building her Forte resource |
| 2 | Fill Forte with basics and Liberation casts | Unlocks her enhanced state |
| 3 | Cast her Ultimate | Drops the healing and Crit buff field |
| 4 | Swap out to your main DPS | Her Outro passes a buff forward |
| 5 | Re-enter on cooldown | Refresh the field before it expires |

Tips that make the loop feel clean:

- Don't leave her on field longer than necessary — her damage contribution is minimal.
- Always swap through her Outro rather than manually switching, so the buff transfers.
- Track the field duration. Recasting the Ultimate while the old field is still up wastes Energy.
- If your Energy Regen is below the recommended breakpoint, you'll feel it immediately as gaps in the buff window.

Honestly, this **Shorekeeper quick guide** would be incomplete without saying it plainly: bad Energy Regen ruins her, no matter how good your Echoes are.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Most **Shorekeeper quick guide** advice focuses on gear, but habits matter just as much. Here are the traps players fall into most often.

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Building Crit instead of Energy Regen | Her buffs scale off Energy Regen, not her own damage | Reroll offensive substats into Energy Regen |
| Skipping the Outro Skill | You lose half her value | Always swap out through her Outro |
| Ulting on cooldown regardless of field uptime | Wastes Energy and delays your next buff | Cast when the previous field is nearly gone |
| Treating her as an on-field unit | Her attacks are weak | Use her as a quick swap-in, swap-out support |
| Ignoring her Inherent Skills | Free healing and Energy Regen left on the table | Level them as soon as they unlock |
| Neglecting her talent levels | Buff and heal values improve with investment | Prioritize her skill and Liberation talents |

## FAQ

**Is Shorekeeper worth building if I already have another healer?**
Usually yes. Her Crit buff and team-wide amplification give her a different job than a pure healer. Many players run her alongside a second sustain unit in the hardest content, and she's a strong upgrade over most alternatives for a single-sustain slot.

**How much Energy Regen does Shorekeeper need?**
Community reports point to a high breakpoint — often cited around 250% — as the point where her Ultimate uptime and buff scaling feel comfortable. Treat that as a target to build toward, not a hard requirement, and check your own rotation for gaps before chasing more.

**Does Shorekeeper need her signature weapon?**
No. Her signature Rectifier is the best option and gives a team damage buff on top of high Energy Regen, but a 4-star Energy Regen Rectifier gets the job done. Build her stats first; upgrade the weapon later if you have the pulls.

**Can a free-to-play account use Shorekeeper effectively?**
Absolutely. Her core value comes from Energy Regen stats, her Echo set, and correct rotation — none of which require premium pulls. A free-to-play Shorekeeper with the Rejuvenating Glow set and a budget Rectifier will outperform a poorly built premium one every time.
