## Shorekeeper Echo Build at a Glance

Shorekeeper is the backbone of most endgame teams in Wuthering Waves, and a sloppy echo loadout quietly drains your healing output and your team's damage. A correct Shorekeeper echo build is the difference between a support who barely keeps everyone alive and one who keeps your main DPS buffed from the first swap to the final hit. Below is a Shorekeeper echo build breakdown covering the sets, main echoes, stats, and farming plan that actually matter.

Shorekeeper is a 5-Star Spectro Resonator who wields a Rectifier and contributes almost no personal damage. Everything she offers — sustained healing, a field that keeps the team topped up, and buffs passed forward through her Outro Skill — scales off HP and Energy Regen. That single fact shapes every echo decision you make on her.

| Decision | Recommended pick | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Echo set | 5-piece Rejuvenating Glow | Healing bonus plus a team-wide ATK buff |
| Main echo (4-cost) | Fallacy of No Return | Team-oriented support effect tied to Energy Regen |
| Backup main echo | Bell-Borne Geochelone | Shield and damage reduction for punishing fights |
| 3-cost main stats | Energy Regen (one or two) | Faster Liberation and Outro cycling |
| 1-cost main stats | HP% | Directly increases her healing |
| Substat priority | Energy Regen → HP% → flat HP | Everything else is close to noise |

If you only remember one line from this guide, make it this: Shorekeeper is not a damage dealer, and building her like one wastes both your echo slots and your Tuners.

## Best Echo Sets for Shorekeeper

Every Resonator equips five echoes under a total cost cap of 12, and sets grant bonuses at two and five pieces. For Shorekeeper, only a handful of sets are worth your resin.

| Echo set | 2-piece bonus | 5-piece bonus (summary) | Run it when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rejuvenating Glow | Healing Bonus | Healing an ally grants the team an ATK buff | Default pick for nearly every team |
| Moonlit Clouds | Energy Regen | Outro Skill buffs the incoming Resonator's ATK | Your DPS benefits from an extra ATK window |
| Tidebreaking Courage | Energy Regen | ATK scales with the wearer's Energy Regen | You can push Energy Regen very high |
| Eternal Radiance | Spectro DMG | Spectro DMG and Crit bonuses | Never on Shorekeeper — this is a DPS set |

**Rejuvenating Glow** is the safe, flexible answer for a Shorekeeper echo build. It reinforces what she already does — healing — and converts that healing into offense for the rest of the team, which is exactly the trade you want from a support slot.

**Moonlit Clouds** is the aggressive alternative. If your main damage dealer already generates their own ATK buffs, stacking another one through Shorekeeper's Outro Skill can push a burst window over a breakpoint.

**Tidebreaking Courage** is a niche but legitimate option for players who can stack Energy Regen to an unusual degree. Community reports suggest it performs best only when your Energy Regen is already near the top of what your gear allows — otherwise you lose more than you gain.

### When to Run a Mixed Set Instead

- **While farming:** two pieces of Rejuvenating Glow plus two pieces of an Energy Regen set is a perfectly serviceable bridge.
- **Short fights:** if your team clears before Shorekeeper's buffs would expire anyway, prioritize raw healing over buff stacking.
- **Co-op or chaotic content:** consistency beats ceiling. Take the set that keeps you alive.

For a full rundown of how echoes, costs, and sets interact, the [Wuthering Waves official site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/) is the authoritative reference.

## Main Echo Choices: Which 4-Cost Echo to Equip

Your main echo is the only slot where the echo skill itself matters, so it deserves more thought than the rest of the loadout. For a Shorekeeper echo build, the choice usually comes down to offense versus safety.

| 4-cost echo | Echo skill (summary) | Best for | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fallacy of No Return | Team-oriented support effect tied to Energy Regen | Buff-focused endgame teams | Less raw survivability |
| Bell-Borne Geochelone | Team shield and damage reduction | Hard bosses, under-geared rosters | No offensive contribution |
| Any 4-cost from your chosen set | Placeholder skill | While you farm the right echo | Wasted slot in serious content |

A standard cost layout for Shorekeeper is **4 / 3 / 3 / 1 / 1**, which exactly fills the 12-point budget and leaves you two 3-cost slots for Energy Regen. Some players experiment with double 4-cost layouts, but that usually costs you the Energy Regen you need to cycle her Ultimate on time.

If you are still assembling the build, equip whatever 4-cost echo drops from the correct set first — the set bonus is worth more than a perfect echo skill in the short term.

## Main Stats and Substats That Actually Matter

Main stats are fixed per slot, so there is very little ambiguity here. The table below reflects the consensus approach for a support-oriented Shorekeeper echo build.

| Slot | Cost | Best main stat | Acceptable alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4-cost | 4 | HP% | Energy Regen |
| 3-cost #1 | 3 | Energy Regen | HP% |
| 3-cost #2 | 3 | Energy Regen | HP% |
| 1-cost #1 | 1 | HP% | Flat HP |
| 1-cost #2 | 1 | HP% | Flat HP |

If you find yourself short on Energy Regen, run two Energy Regen 3-costs. Once you are comfortably above your target, swapping one to HP% gives you noticeably bigger heals without hurting your rotation.

### Substat Priority

| Priority | Substat | What it does for Shorekeeper |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Regen | More frequent Liberation and Outro Skills = more buff uptime |
| 2 | HP% | Larger heals across the board |
| 3 | Flat HP | Small but essentially free healing increase |
| 4 | Crit Rate / Crit DMG | Only relevant if you have leftover rolls and nothing better |
| Skip | ATK%, Spectro DMG, Basic/Heavy Attack DMG | Her kit does not scale on these |

Player experience consistently points to Energy Regen as the stat that makes or breaks a Shorekeeper echo build. Community reports commonly cite a range around 200%–250% Energy Regen as comfortable for keeping her field and buffs online, though the exact comfortable number shifts with team composition and balance patches. Treat that as a starting target, then test in the training room rather than trusting any single number.

A practical tip: never tune a 3-cost echo that rolled flat ATK or Spectro DMG. Those rolls are dead weight on Shorekeeper, and Tuners are too scarce to waste.

## Farming Plan, Rotation Tips, and Common Mistakes

Farming for Shorekeeper is straightforward once you know what you are looking for. The table below is a step-by-step plan you can follow in order.

| Step | Action | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Farm the boss that drops your chosen 4-cost echo | Repeat until you get the right set, not just the right echo |
| 2 | Collect 3-cost echoes from overworld clusters | Group spawns and clear routes rather than wandering |
| 3 | Fill the last two slots with 1-costs | These are the cheapest slots to replace later |
| 4 | Level to +25 only after the first substats look usable | Do not sink resources into a bad roll |
| 5 | Tune substats in batches | Stop as soon as you have two Energy Regen rolls |
| 6 | Recycle failed echoes through the data bank | Free rerolls beat vendor trash |

### Rotation Habits That Protect Your Build

- Enter the field, drop your healing field, and leave. Shorekeeper's value comes from being off-field while her buffs tick.
- Use her Outro Skill as your swap trigger so the buff lands on the Resonator who actually deals damage.
- Do not chase damage windows with her. Every second she spends attacking is a second your carry is not.
- Keep her Ultimate available for the moment your team takes chip damage, not for the opener.

### Team Fit

| Team archetype | Shorekeeper's job | Echo priority |
|---|---|---|
| Hypercarry DPS | Buff and heal, hold the field | Maximum Energy Regen, team ATK buffs |
| Quickswap dual DPS | Maintain field uptime between swaps | Energy Regen plus healing bonus |
| Survival / hard mode | Shield and sustain through one-shots | Bell-Borne Geochelone main echo |

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Building Crit on a healer.** It looks impressive in the stat sheet and does almost nothing in practice.
- **Ignoring Energy Regen.** A Shorekeeper who cannot cycle her Ultimate is a wasted team slot.
- **Over-investing in 1-cost echoes.** Replace them last, not first.
- **Running a Spectro DMG set.** Eternal Radiance belongs on your Spectro damage dealer, not on her.
- **Copying a build blindly.** Team composition changes which buffs are redundant.

## FAQ

**Is a Shorekeeper echo build expensive to finish?**
Compared with a DPS build, no. She only needs two or three specific stats, so you can stop tuning early once Energy Regen and HP% appear. That makes her one of the cheapest supports to gear properly.

**Should I use Fallacy of No Return or Bell-Borne Geochelone as my main echo?**
Use Fallacy of No Return when your team is comfortable surviving and you want more buff pressure. Switch to Bell-Borne Geochelone when a fight punishes mistakes or your gear is still developing.

**How much Energy Regen does Shorekeeper need?**
Player experience suggests aiming high — many community reports land in the 200%–250% range — but the right number depends on your team's rotation. Start high, then trade Energy Regen for HP% only after your Ultimate comes back on schedule.

**Does a Shorekeeper echo build change in co-op?**
The priorities stay the same: Energy Regen first, HP% second. In co-op you may want a defensive main echo, since you cannot rely on teammates to dodge for you.
