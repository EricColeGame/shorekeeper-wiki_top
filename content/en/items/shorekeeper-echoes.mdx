## Why Shorekeeper Echoes Decide Her Real Power

Building Shorekeeper is less about making her hit harder and more about making everyone else hit harder — and that starts with the right echoes. If your **Shorekeeper echoes** are sloppy, her healing still functions, but the team-wide buffs that define her role never reach their ceiling. This guide breaks down the best sets, main stats, and substat priorities so your **Shorekeeper echoes** actually amplify the way she plays instead of fighting against it.

Shorekeeper is a 5-star Spectro Resonator who wields a Rectifier and fills a healer-support slot. Her personal damage is low by design. What she brings instead is sustained healing plus offensive buffs that carry forward into whichever character she hands the field to. Every echo decision should therefore be judged against one question: does this make the team stronger, or does it just pad her own numbers?

Short answer: build for team value, not for her stat sheet.

## How the Echo System Shapes a Shorekeeper Build

Echoes are Wuthering Waves' gear system. Each Resonator equips up to five echoes, and the combined cost of those five cannot exceed 12. Echoes come in three cost tiers, and each tier rolls from a different pool of main stats — which is exactly why Shorekeeper builds look so different from a main DPS build.

| Cost tier | Typical slots | Main stats available | Role for Shorekeeper |
| 4-cost | 1 | Crit Rate, Crit DMG, ATK%, HP%, Healing Bonus, Energy Regen | Carries the strongest main echo skill |
| 3-cost | 2 | Elemental DMG bonus, ATK%, HP%, Energy Regen | Best place to patch Energy Regen or stack HP% |
| 1-cost | 2 | ATK%, HP%, DEF% | Cheap, reliable HP% padding |

Two layouts dominate. The **4-3-3-1-1** spread gives you two 3-cost echoes, which is the most flexible option because it lets you run Energy Regen on one and HP% on the other. The **4-3-1-1-1** spread trades a 3-cost slot for an extra 1-cost echo, and it is only worth considering if you already have an outstanding HP% roll sitting in your inventory.

For a support whose healing scales off HP and whose ultimate needs steady Energy Regen, the 4-3-3-1-1 layout is usually the safer default.

## Best Echo Sets for Shorekeeper

Set bonuses matter more than raw stats here. Two sets are genuinely competitive, and a third works as a stopgap while you farm.

| Echo set | 2-piece bonus | 5-piece bonus | When to use it |
| Rejuvenating Glow | Healing Bonus +10% | After healing a teammate, all team members gain ATK for 30s | Default pick for most accounts |
| Moonlit Clouds | Energy Regen +10% | After an Outro Skill, the incoming Resonator gains a large ATK boost | Quick-swap teams and Outro-heavy rotations |
| Rejuvenating Glow 2 + Moonlit Clouds 2 | Healing Bonus +10% and Energy Regen +10% | — | Budget placeholder while you farm |

**Rejuvenating Glow** is the standard healer set, and Shorekeeper triggers it almost effortlessly. Her healing field pulses continuously, so the 5-piece team ATK buff has near-permanent uptime in practice. If you want one set to farm and then forget about, this is the one.

**Moonlit Clouds** is the more aggressive choice. Its 5-piece bonus fires on Outro Skill use and funnels a larger ATK buff into the character you swap to. In teams that rotate quickly — where Shorekeeper is briefly on field before handing off to a main DPS — the timing lines up beautifully.

Which one wins? It depends on your rotation. Player experience suggests Rejuvenating Glow is more forgiving, while Moonlit Clouds rewards tighter play. If you are unsure, start with Rejuvenating Glow and branch out later.

## Picking the Right Main Echo

The 4-cost slot is where your build gets its signature. Several options are worth weighing.

| Main echo | What it provides | Best for |
| Fallacy of No Return | A team-wide ATK buff triggered by casting your Resonance Skill | Almost every support-oriented team |
| Bell-Borne Geochelone | A shield and damage reduction for the active character | Aggressive bosses, co-op, and learning new fights |
| Mourning Aix | Spectro DMG and an ATK boost | Only if you deliberately want personal damage |
| Any 4-cost with Energy Regen | Pure stat stick | Temporary filler while you farm the real thing |

**Fallacy of No Return** is the consensus pick because its buff stacks with your set bonus instead of competing with it. You end up layering two separate team-wide ATK boosts, which is exactly what a support wants.

**Bell-Borne Geochelone** is the survivability answer. If your team keeps dying to a specific boss, swapping to it costs you some offense but buys a lot of breathing room. That is a legitimate trade, not a meme build.

Avoid building Shorekeeper around **Mourning Aix**. Her damage multipliers are low, so Spectro DMG main stats and Crit substats do very little for her compared to what they would do on a real damage dealer.

## Main Stats and Substat Priority

Lock in main stats first, because they are guaranteed. Substats are where the grinding actually lives.

| Slot | First choice | Second choice | Avoid |
| 4-cost | Energy Regen | Healing Bonus | Crit Rate, Crit DMG |
| 3-cost (x2) | Energy Regen | HP% | Elemental DMG bonus |
| 1-cost (x2) | HP% | Flat HP | ATK%, DEF% |

For substats, think in tiers rather than chasing perfection:

| Priority | Substat | Reasoning |
| 1 | Energy Regen | Directly controls how often her Liberation is available |
| 2 | HP% | Scales her healing output |
| 3 | Flat HP | Small but always useful |
| 4 | Crit Rate / Crit DMG | Only matters if you are chasing personal damage |
| 5 | ATK% / DEF% | Nearly dead weight on a support build |

The single most common mistake is treating Shorekeeper like a damage dealer. Community reports repeatedly show that players who stack Crit on her end up with a healer who neither heals noticeably better nor contributes meaningful damage. Energy Regen and HP% are the stats that actually change how the character feels to pilot.

A practical rule of thumb: if your Energy Regen is short, fix that before you chase anything else. Ultimate uptime is the backbone of her kit, and no amount of HP% compensates for a Liberation that is never ready.

## Farming Shorekeeper Echoes Efficiently

Echo farming runs mostly through Tacet Fields, which cost waveplates and drop echoes from one specific set. Here is a workflow that saves both time and resources:

- **Pick your set first.** Decide between Rejuvenating Glow and Moonlit Clouds before you spend a single waveplate, because you cannot farm both efficiently at once.
- **Run the matching Tacet Field.** Each clear targets one set, so commit to a field rather than bouncing between them.
- **Level to +5, then check.** Cheap early upgrades reveal whether a substat roll is worth continuing. Do not dump resources into an echo that already missed Energy Regen and HP%.
- **Use Echo Merge on junk.** Duplicates and off-set drops can be converted rather than discarded.
- **Keep gold-rarity echoes only** once your Data Bank is high enough to drop them consistently. Lower rarities cap out too low to matter long term.
- **Stop at "good enough."** A support does not need perfect rolls. Two or three useful substats per echo is a perfectly functional build.

If you are new to the game and want to confirm current systems before committing resources, the [official Wuthering Waves site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/en/main) is the authoritative place to check.

## Rotation and Team Notes

Shorekeeper's value spikes when her kit is sequenced correctly. The general shape is: enter, establish her healing field and buffs, then use her Outro Skill to hand the field to your main damage dealer. The Outro Skill is what carries the team buff forward, so swapping out at the wrong moment wastes it.

She fits into nearly any composition that needs a healer with offensive upside. In quick-swap teams, Moonlit Clouds shines because Outro Skills fire constantly. In slower single-DPS teams, Rejuvenating Glow's continuous healing trigger keeps the ATK buff rolling without you having to think about it.

One more note on co-op: if you are playing Shorekeeper in multiplayer, survivability echoes like Bell-Borne Geochelone become more attractive, because you cannot always rely on teammates to dodge properly. Once your Shorekeeper echoes are settled, the rest of the build is mostly about learning your team's swap rhythm.

## FAQ

**What is the best echo set for Shorekeeper?**
Rejuvenating Glow is the safest all-around answer for Shorekeeper echoes, thanks to its healing-triggered team ATK buff. Moonlit Clouds is equally viable and slightly stronger in fast rotations where her Outro Skill fires often. Either is a legitimate endgame choice.

**Which main stat should the 4-cost Shorekeeper echo have?**
Energy Regen is the most practical pick for most players, because it keeps her Liberation available. Healing Bonus and HP% are reasonable alternatives if you already have Energy Regen covered on your 3-cost echoes.

**Do Shorekeeper echoes need Crit Rate or Crit DMG?**
Generally, no. Her damage multipliers are low, so Crit stats contribute very little compared to Energy Regen and HP%. Only chase Crit if you have already maxed out her support stats and want a personal-damage project.

**How much Energy Regen does Shorekeeper need?**
Community reports vary by team and rotation, but the consistent advice is to prioritize Energy Regen above every other substat until her ultimate feels reliably available. Test your own rotation and adjust from there rather than copying a fixed number.
