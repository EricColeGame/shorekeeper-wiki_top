Shorekeeper is one of the most valuable supports you can build in Wuthering Waves, and she is worth every resource you pour into her — but the full set of **Shorekeeper materials** needed to take her from a fresh pull to a level 90 healer is not small. Start farming without a plan and you will burn Waveplate on the wrong upgrades while waiting weeks on a weekly boss you forgot to claim.

This guide breaks down every category of **Shorekeeper materials**, flags which ones are time-gated, and lays out the farming order that gets her combat-ready fastest. Whether you are pre-farming ahead of a rerun banner or finishing a half-built support, the plan below will save you a lot of wasted stamina.

## Shorekeeper Materials at a Glance

Every Resonator in Wuthering Waves is built from the same handful of material categories, and Shorekeeper is no exception. What changes between characters is *which* boss drop, *which* local specialty, and *which* enemy drop line you need.

| Material Category | What It's Used For | Where It Comes From | Time-Gated? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ascension boss material | All six ascension phases (Lv. 20 → 90) | Overworld boss challenge | No |
| Local specialty | All six ascension phases | Open-world collection, respawn cycle | Partly — respawn timer |
| Common enemy drop line (low / mid / high tier) | Ascension + Forte upgrades | Overworld enemies, combat domains | No |
| Forgery Challenge materials | Forte (skill tree) upgrades | Forgery Challenge | No |
| Weekly boss material | Higher-tier Forte upgrades | Weekly Challenge | **Yes** — limited claims per week |
| Shell Credits | Every upgrade in the game | Simulation Training, quests, events | No, but drains fast |

The exact item names for each slot are printed directly on Shorekeeper's Ascension and Forte screens in-game. Drop tables and material names are occasionally adjusted between patches, so confirm the current list before committing Waveplate — and keep an eye on the [official Wuthering Waves website](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/en/main) for patch notes that shift farming routes.

## Ascension Materials: Taking Shorekeeper to Level 90

Ascension is the level-gate half of the grind. Shorekeeper ascends at Lv. 20, 40, 50, 60, 70, and 80, with a hard cap of level 90 after the sixth phase. Each phase consumes four things: a boss material, a local specialty, common enemy drops, and Shell Credits.

The quantities grow with each phase, so the last two ascensions are by far the most expensive. That is also where the return on investment drops — the stat gain from 80 → 90 is real, but it costs more than the entire early game combined.

| Ascension Phase | Unlocks | Categories Consumed | Prep Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Lv. 20 → 40 | Boss drop, specialty, low-tier drops, Shell Credits | Cheapest phase — do it immediately |
| 2nd | Lv. 40 → 50 | Same categories, larger amounts | Start specialty runs now |
| 3rd | Lv. 50 → 60 | Same categories, larger amounts | Bank Shell Credits in advance |
| 4th | Lv. 60 → 70 | Same categories, larger amounts | Mid-tier drops become the bottleneck |
| 5th | Lv. 70 → 80 | Same categories, largest mid-game cost | This unlocks higher Forte levels — prioritize it |
| 6th | Lv. 80 → 90 | Same categories, highest cost | Do this last; smallest gain per Waveplate |

Two practical notes. First, local specialties respawn on a fixed cycle and only a limited number exist per region, so treat them as a daily habit rather than one long grind session. Second, common enemy drops are the only material on this list you can farm "for free" — you will collect them naturally while doing dailies, exploring, and clearing combat content.

## Forte Materials: Which Skills to Upgrade First

Forte is Shorekeeper's skill tree: Basic Attack, Resonance Skill, Resonance Liberation, Forte Circuit, Intro Skill, plus two Inherent Skills. Ability levels climb toward 10, and the top tiers are where weekly boss materials and Forgery Challenge drops disappear fast.

Community reports consistently agree on one thing: Shorekeeper is a support, not an on-field damage dealer, so her Basic Attack is the lowest priority node in her entire kit.

| Forte Node | Priority | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Resonance Liberation | Highest | Core healing plus the team-wide Crit buff window |
| Forte Circuit | High | Central to how her kit actually functions |
| Resonance Skill | Medium-high | Feeds her rotation and healing uptime |
| Intro Skill | Medium | Useful, but not where her value lives |
| Basic Attack | Lowest | She is rarely the one swinging |
| Both Inherent Skills | Unlock ASAP | Cheap, permanent passives for very little material |

The weekly boss material is the true bottleneck here. You can only claim a limited number of Weekly Challenge rewards each week, and that cap does not care whether you have finished farming everything else. Claim those rewards every single week, even before you own the character.

## Waveplate Math: How Long Does Farming Actually Take?

Waveplate is the resource that decides your farming pace. At the standard cap, you regenerate roughly one Waveplate every six minutes — around 240 per day if you never let it overflow. Here is how the main activities compare.

| Activity | Typical Waveplate Cost | What You Get | Efficiency Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly Challenge | 60 per claim, limited claims weekly | Weekly boss material | Never skip — hard time gate |
| Boss Challenge | 60 per claim | Ascension boss material | Batch these runs into one session |
| Forgery Challenge | 40 per run | Forte materials | Best value per Waveplate for skills |
| Simulation Training | 40 per run | Shell Credits / EXP | Run when credits run dry |
| Tacet Field | 60 per run | Echoes | Farm after character materials |

The takeaway is simple: a single day of Waveplate buys you roughly four boss claims or six Forgery Challenge runs. If you spread that across an entire character build without a priority order, you will feel like you are making no progress at all. Pick one target per day.

## A Step-by-Step Farming Plan for Shorekeeper Materials

Here is the order that minimizes wasted Waveplate. It is built around the one thing you cannot speed up: the weekly lockout.

| Priority | Task | Why It Comes First |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claim Weekly Challenge rewards every week | Hard time gate — you cannot catch up later |
| 2 | Unlock both Inherent Skills | Cheap, permanent, immediate value |
| 3 | Farm common enemy drops passively during dailies | Costs no extra Waveplate |
| 4 | Ascend to Lv. 80 | Unlocks the higher Forte levels |
| 5 | Push key Forte nodes toward max | Biggest power spike per material spent |
| 6 | Finish the 80 → 90 ascension | Real gain, but the worst return per Waveplate |

If you are pre-farming Shorekeeper materials before a banner, front-load steps 1 through 3. Weekly boss materials and common drops are the two categories you can stockpile without knowing your final build, and they are the two most likely to slow you down later.

## Common Mistakes When Farming Shorekeeper Materials

A few habits separate players who finish a build in two weeks from players who take two months:

- **Letting Waveplate sit at cap.** Every hour at maximum is regeneration you never get back.
- **Skipping weekly claims.** There is no make-up mechanic. Miss three weeks and your Forte upgrades slip by three weeks.
- **Over-investing in Basic Attack.** Materials spent there are materials not spent on her Liberation.
- **Grinding specialties in one sitting.** Respawn timers mean a 15-minute daily route beats a three-hour binge.
- **Ascending to 90 before leveling Forte.** The ascension unlocks the ceiling; the Forte nodes are what actually make her strong.
- **Ignoring Shell Credits until you are broke.** Credits fund every single upgrade. Keep a buffer.

## FAQ

**What are the main Shorekeeper materials I need to farm?**
You need four categories for ascension — an ascension boss drop, a local specialty, a common enemy drop line, and Shell Credits — plus Forgery Challenge materials and a weekly boss material for her Forte tree. The exact item names are listed on her Ascension and Forte screens in-game.

**Can I pre-farm Shorekeeper materials before I pull her?**
Yes, and you should. Weekly boss materials and common enemy drops can be stockpiled with no downside, since they are used across multiple characters. Boss materials and specialties tied specifically to her are also safe to farm early — just confirm the current list first.

**How long does it take to fully build Shorekeeper?**
The weekly lockout is the real limit, not Waveplate. Most players report that a few weeks of consistent weekly claims is the deciding factor, while the Waveplate-based categories can be cleared in a handful of focused farming days.

**Do I need to max every Forte node?**
No. Maxing Resonance Liberation, Forte Circuit, and unlocking both Inherent Skills delivers the overwhelming majority of her value. Basic Attack can stay low indefinitely without hurting your team.
