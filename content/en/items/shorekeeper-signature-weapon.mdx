Shorekeeper is one of the most impactful support units in Wuthering Waves, and her performance hinges on a stat most players neglect: Energy Regen. That single fact explains why the **Shorekeeper signature weapon**, Stellar Symphony, sits at the top of nearly every build guide you'll find. If you want her team-wide crit buffs to reach their ceiling, the weapon she was designed around is usually the fastest route there — but it isn't the only route, and it isn't always the smartest purchase. This guide breaks down what the **Shorekeeper signature weapon** actually does, how the weapon banner works, what it costs, and how to decide whether it deserves your pulls.

## Why Shorekeeper's Kit Makes Her Weapon Choice So Important

Shorekeeper is a 5-star Spectro support who wields a Rectifier. Almost everything she contributes flows through her Resonance Liberation, which creates a field that heals your team and grants crit buffs to everyone standing inside it. That kind of buff quietly raises your entire squad's damage ceiling in endgame content such as the Tower of Adversity and high-difficulty hologram fights.

The catch is that her buffs scale with **Energy Regen**, not with her personal damage output. Build her like a damage dealer and you get a mediocre healer with weak buffs. Build her for Energy Regen and ultimate uptime and she becomes one of the strongest support units in the game. That single design choice is the reason the Shorekeeper signature weapon conversation exists at all.

### What She Actually Needs From a Weapon

Because her kit is buff-driven rather than damage-driven, the value of any Rectifier on Shorekeeper comes down to three questions: Does it give Energy Regen? Does it help her ultimate come back faster? Does it add anything to the team beyond her own numbers?

A weapon that answers all three is worth far more on her than a higher-rarity stat stick that only boosts her personal damage. Community reports consistently show that players who chase raw attack on Shorekeeper end up with slower rotations and noticeably weaker buffs.

| Priority | Goal | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Regen | Directly scales her team buffs and ultimate uptime |
| 2 | Ultimate uptime | Her entire value lives inside her Liberation field |
| 3 | Team-wide damage support | Any passive that buffs allies is a bonus |
| 4 | Personal damage | Nice to have, but rarely the deciding factor |

## Stellar Symphony: What the Shorekeeper Signature Weapon Actually Does

Stellar Symphony is the 5-star Rectifier released alongside Shorekeeper, and it is built around the exact stat her kit wants. Rather than treating her as a damage dealer, the weapon leans into Energy Regen and support-oriented passives, which keeps her Liberation cycling and her buffs active through long fights.

In practice, this means smoother rotations. You spend less time waiting on her ultimate and more time with the crit buff up on your main damage dealer — which is where the real damage gain comes from. For a support unit, "more uptime" is usually worth more than "bigger numbers."

### What to Look for in a Substitute

If you don't pull Stellar Symphony, you're not locked out of playing Shorekeeper effectively. You simply need to solve the same problem with a different tool. The checklist is short:

- **Energy Regen on the substat.** This is non-negotiable if you want her buffs near their cap.
- **A passive that helps the team.** Anything that buffs allies or restores energy beats a purely selfish damage passive.
- **Enough base attack to matter.** Low-rarity weapons can leave her healing feeling thin in harder content.

| Weapon Option | Rarity | Best For | Trade-Off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stellar Symphony | 5-star | Maxing Energy Regen and buff uptime | Costs pulls on the weapon banner |
| Other 5-star Rectifiers | 5-star | Players who already own one | Usually crit-focused, less Energy Regen |
| 4-star Energy Regen Rectifiers | 4-star | Budget and free-to-play builds | Lower base stats, weaker passive |
| Any leftover Rectifier | 3–4 star | Early progression only | Won't reach her buff ceiling |

Community-favorite budget picks like Jinzhou Keeper and Variation come up constantly in Shorekeeper discussions, and they work well enough to clear most content — they just leave some buff value on the table compared to the signature option.

## How to Get the Shorekeeper Signature Weapon

Stellar Symphony is not available on the standard banner. It appears on the Weapon Event Convene, which uses **Forging Tides** and typically runs at the same time as Shorekeeper's character banner during a rerun. If her banner isn't active, the weapon isn't either — so timing matters as much as saving.

The good news is that the weapon banner in Wuthering Waves is far more forgiving than most gacha weapon systems. The featured 5-star weapon is guaranteed, with no 50/50 coin flip to lose. You know exactly what you're buying into, which makes planning genuinely possible.

| Pulls | Forging Tides Needed | Astrite Equivalent | Scenario |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 10 | 1,600 | Lucky early hit |
| 40 | 40 | 6,400 | Around the average outcome |
| 60 | 60 | 9,600 | Slightly unlucky |
| 80 | 80 | 12,800 | Hard pity, worst case |

Those figures assume the standard conversion rate of 160 Astrite per pull. Always confirm current numbers on the [official Wuthering Waves site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/) or in the in-game Convene screen, since banner rules and rewards can shift between versions.

### Pity Rules You Should Know

- Weapon banner pity is tracked separately from character banner pity. Pulls on one do not help the other.
- Pity carries over between Weapon Event Convenes, so a partial investment is never wasted.
- The featured weapon is guaranteed, which removes the worst-case "lost the 50/50" scenario entirely.
- Forging Tides are the only currency that works on this banner — Astrite converts, but Lustrous and Radiant Tides do not.

## Is the Shorekeeper Signature Weapon Worth Pulling?

This is where most players overthink things. The honest answer is that the Shorekeeper signature weapon is a **comfort and ceiling upgrade**, not a mandatory one. If your Shorekeeper is already hitting her Energy Regen target with a 4-star weapon and good Echo substats, the signature weapon mostly buys you flexibility — you can shift some Energy Regen rolls into other stats without losing buff uptime.

If she's underperforming, though, the weapon is often the cheapest fix available. Rerolling Echoes for Energy Regen can take weeks of farming. A guaranteed weapon takes a fixed, predictable number of pulls.

| Your Situation | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Brand-new account with no built DPS | Skip — build your damage core first |
| Shorekeeper is your main support and you have 12,800+ Astrite banked | Pull — it's a guaranteed, permanent upgrade |
| You already own a spare 5-star Rectifier | Test it first, then decide |
| Saving for an upcoming character you want more | Skip — the weapon will rerun |
| You're stuck below your Energy Regen target | Pull, or farm better Echoes as a free alternative |

### The Opportunity Cost Question

Every pull on a weapon banner is a pull you can't spend on a character. That trade-off matters more than any stat comparison. Ask yourself one question: does Shorekeeper's buff uptime matter more to your account right now than a new playable character? If the answer is no, skip the weapon, keep farming Echoes, and revisit on the next rerun. The banner will come back.

## Alternatives, Build Tips, and Upgrade Priority

Whether or not you pull the signature, the build process is the same. Get her Energy Regen high, keep her Liberation cycling, and let your main damage dealer do the work.

| Upgrade Step | Priority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Level the Rectifier to max | High | Base stats scale everything else |
| Push Energy Regen on Echoes | High | The single biggest performance lever |
| Equip a healing-oriented Echo set | High | Sets like Rejuvenating Glow support her role |
| Level Forte and Liberation nodes | Medium | Improves healing and buff consistency |
| Optimize personal damage | Low | Only after everything above is done |

A few practical tips from player experience:

- **Test before you commit.** Run the same boss fight with and without the signature weapon. If your clear time barely moves, your Astrite is better spent elsewhere.
- **Don't chase 100% perfect Echo rolls.** Getting within a few percent of your Energy Regen target is usually enough; the last few rolls cost far more than they return.
- **Keep a rotation habit.** Shorekeeper's value depends on her field being active during your damage window. A perfect weapon won't save a mistimed rotation.
- **Watch rerun timing.** Weapon banners return with their characters, so there's rarely a reason to panic-pull.
