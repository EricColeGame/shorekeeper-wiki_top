Shorekeeper is one of the few Wuthering Waves supports whose weapon slot genuinely changes how the rest of your team performs. Because her signature team buffs scale off Energy Regen, the best Shorekeeper weapon is rarely the one with the biggest raw numbers — it's the one that pushes her past a stat breakpoint and hands your main DPS free CRIT stats. Pick the wrong Shorekeeper weapon and you'll spend the rest of your build farming energy that a single substat could have provided.

This guide covers every realistic option, from her signature 5-star Rectifier down to the 4-star fillers you probably already own, along with the stat targets and rotation notes that make each pick actually work in practice.

## Why the Weapon Slot Matters So Much for Shorekeeper

Shorekeeper fills the healer/support slot as a Spectro Rectifier user. Her job is twofold: keep the team alive, and convert her Energy Regen into team-wide CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG buffs through her field.

That second part is the important one. Most supports scale their buffs off ATK or a flat percentage baked into their kit. Shorekeeper instead ties her buff ceiling to Energy Regen, which means a weapon with an Energy Regen substat isn't a convenience — it is a direct damage increase for your carry.

The practical result is simple: a weapon that looks "weak" because it has no CRIT or ATK substat can still be her best-in-slot, while a shiny 5-star DPS Rectifier can be close to dead weight.

| Weapon feature | What it does for Shorekeeper | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Energy Regen substat | Feeds her CRIT buff conversion and reduces ER pressure from Echoes | Essential |
| Team-wide ATK or DMG passive | Direct damage increase for your main DPS | High |
| Concerto / energy-restore passives | Faster Liberation cycles and smoother field uptime | Medium-High |
| Healing or HP passive | Smoother survivability in long fights | Medium |
| Raw ATK, CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG | Barely used — she is not a damage dealer | Low |

### Stellar Symphony (Signature)

Stellar Symphony is Shorekeeper's signature 5-star Rectifier and the benchmark every other option is measured against. It combines an Energy Regen substat with a passive that supports the team rather than the wielder, which is exactly the profile a support weapon should have.

If you already pulled it, your build problems are largely solved. If you haven't, understand what you're paying for: convenience and a higher buff ceiling, not a mandatory unlock. Shorekeeper clears all current endgame content without it.

### Jinzhou Keeper

Jinzhou Keeper is the closest thing to a free answer. As a standard-banner 5-star Rectifier with an Energy Regen substat, it gives Shorekeeper the stat she cares about most plus a team-oriented passive.

For most players who don't want to chase a limited weapon banner, this is the realistic endgame target. Community reports consistently place it a clear step behind Stellar Symphony but comfortably ahead of every 4-star option.

### Variation

Variation is the classic budget Rectifier and the weapon most free-to-play accounts already have lying around. Its Energy Regen substat is the entire point — it lets Shorekeeper reach her ER breakpoint without sacrificing every Echo roll to energy.

Player experience suggests a well-refined Variation is enough to clear the hardest content in the game. The gap between it and a 5-star option shows up in clear speed and comfort, not in whether the team functions at all.

### Cosmic Ripples, Stringmaster, and Other DPS Rectifiers

These are 5-star weapons with ATK or CRIT substats and passives aimed at damage dealers. They will make Shorekeeper's own numbers look better on a stat sheet, but they do nothing for the buffs your team actually receives.

There's also an opportunity cost: equipping Stringmaster on Shorekeeper means your Rectifier DPS isn't using it. Unless you genuinely have no Energy Regen option, leave these where they belong.

### Call of the Abyss and Early-Game Fillers

Call of the Abyss and similar ATK-substat 4-stars are fine for the first few weeks of an account, before you've farmed proper Echoes. Once you can hit your Energy Regen target through gear, swap to anything with an ER substat, even a lower-rarity weapon.

## Stat Targets for a Shorekeeper Build

Weapon choice and Echo choice are the same conversation. A stronger weapon means fewer Energy Regen rolls needed on your Echoes, which frees those rolls for HP and survivability.

The targets below reflect widely accepted community consensus rather than official documentation. Verify current values in-game, since balance adjustments can shift breakpoints between patches.

| Stat | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Energy Regen | Roughly 250% total | Caps her CRIT buff conversion |
| HP% | As high as possible after ER | Feeds her healing output |
| Flat HP | Filler | Small incremental healing gain |
| CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG | Ignore | She deals negligible damage |
| ATK% | Low priority | Doesn't scale her support kit |

### The Order of Operations

1. Hit your Energy Regen breakpoint first — everything else is secondary.
2. Fill remaining slots with HP% for healing throughput.
3. Only then consider flat HP or minor defensive stats.
4. Never sacrifice Energy Regen for CRIT substats on a support.

Most players don't need to decide between the best and worst option in a vacuum. They need to know what to use right now. This table maps account stage to a sensible Shorekeeper weapon.

| Account stage | Recommended weapon | Reasoning |

The honest takeaway: Shorekeeper's weapon is a comfort and ceiling upgrade, not a gate. Spending your limited pulls on a main DPS weapon will usually improve your account more than upgrading her Rectifier.

## Team and Rotation Notes That Change Weapon Value

Weapon passives that trigger on Resonance Skill or Liberation use depend on how often Shorekeeper is actually on field. She is a quick-swap support, so passives requiring long field time are wasted.

- Pair her with a main DPS who benefits from CRIT buffs — most do.
- Keep her field active during your carry's burst window.
- Generate Concerto efficiently so her Liberation comes back on cooldown.
- Don't over-invest in her personal damage; the team buff is the payoff.

## FAQ

**Is Stellar Symphony required for Shorekeeper?**
No. It's the strongest Shorekeeper weapon, but every piece of current endgame content is clearable with a refined 4-star Energy Regen Rectifier. Treat the signature as an upgrade, not a requirement.

**What's the best free-to-play option?**
Variation is the standard answer. Its Energy Regen substat does the heavy lifting, and it's widely available from standard pulls. Any other Rectifier with an Energy Regen substat works as a substitute.

**Should I put a 5-star DPS Rectifier on her instead?**
Generally no. Those weapons boost personal damage, which Shorekeeper doesn't need, while leaving your actual damage dealer without their best option. The only exception is when you have literally nothing else.

**How much Energy Regen should I aim for?**
Community consensus points to roughly 250% total Energy Regen to cap her CRIT buff conversion. Your weapon substat is the cheapest source of that stat, which is exactly why it dominates every Shorekeeper weapon recommendation.

**Does the weapon matter more than her Echoes?**
They work together. A strong Energy Regen weapon reduces how many ER rolls your Echoes need, letting you invest those rolls into HP. Start with the weapon, then tune the Echoes around it.
