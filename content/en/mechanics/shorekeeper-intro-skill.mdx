## Intro Skills 101: The Swap Mechanic Behind Shorekeeper's Kit

Every Resonator in Wuthering Waves has an entry move that fires automatically when they are swapped onto the field while the outgoing character has a full Concerto bar. You do not press a button for it. You *earn* it by managing the previous character's field time — which is why Intro Skills reward players who think in rotations rather than in single characters.

That distinction matters a lot for Shorekeeper, because her kit is spread across four separate ability slots, and only one of them is the one you control directly.

| Ability slot | How it fires | What it costs | What it typically does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intro Skill | Auto-triggers when you swap her in with a full Concerto bar | Nothing directly; depends on the outgoing character's Outro | Entry damage plus a team heal |
| Resonance Skill | Manual cast while she is on field | Cooldown | Core kit effect and healing |
| Resonance Liberation | Manual cast with full Resonance Energy | Energy | Creates her signature field and team buffs |
| Outro Skill | Auto-triggers when she swaps out with a full Concerto bar | Her Concerto bar | Buffs and support for the incoming character |

Because Shorekeeper is a support, her value is not concentrated in a single nuke. It is spread across all four slots, and the Intro Skill is the piece that keeps the whole rotation from stalling. If you want the official wording on how these slots are defined, the [official Wuthering Waves site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/) is the place to check, since ability descriptions there are always the final authority.

## What the Shorekeeper Intro Skill Actually Does

At its core, Shorekeeper's entry move is a hybrid: it deals Spectro damage as she arrives and restores health to the team. That combination is deceptively strong, because it means her swap-in is never a dead turn — she contributes chip damage while resetting your team's health bars.

- **Spectro damage on entry.** The numbers are modest compared with a dedicated DPS, but they are free damage that happens while you are already doing something else.
- **Team-wide healing.** Community reports consistently describe her Intro Skill as the moment her healing touches the whole party, not just the active character.
- **Concerto and energy progress.** Every Intro Skill feeds the next swap, which keeps the rotation's rhythm intact.
- **Instant repositioning.** Swapping in briefly moves her out of the path of incoming attacks, which makes it a legitimate defensive tool.

Exact multipliers and scaling should always be confirmed in the in-game skill menu, because balance adjustments happen and tooltips are the only source that stays current.

### The hidden value is tempo, not healing

A heal that costs zero field time is worth far more than a bigger heal that costs three seconds of your main damage dealer's uptime. The Shorekeeper Intro Skill is essentially a free reset button: your team gets topped up, your support gets on field, and your damage dealer loses almost nothing in the exchange.

### It does not replace her Liberation

Her Resonance Liberation is what creates the field that carries her signature team buffs. The Intro Skill is the connective tissue between those bursts — the thing that keeps everyone alive and in position while you wait for energy to come back. Treat them as partners, not substitutes.

## How to Trigger the Shorekeeper Intro Skill Reliably

The single most common reason players see no Intro Skill is simple: they swapped too early. If the outgoing character's Concerto bar is not full, the swap is a plain switch with no entry animation at all.

| Step | Action | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Play your sub-DPS or main DPS until their Concerto bar fills | The ring around the character portrait glowing |
| 2 | Swap to Shorekeeper | Her Intro Skill animation plays the moment she lands |
| 3 | Let the heal resolve before swapping again | Party HP bars jumping, not just the active character's |
| 4 | Swap to your main DPS | Their Outro-granted buff applying on entry |
| 5 | Repeat the loop | Concerto refilling on the character you just left |

A few habits that make this reliable:

- **Watch the portrait ring, not the health bar.** Concerto readiness is the only thing that matters for whether the Intro Skill fires.
- **Do not mash the swap button.** Queue your swap once the bar is visibly full; spamming it can trigger a plain switch during a recovery animation.
- **Treat her entry as a rotation beat, not an emergency button.** If you only ever bring her in when someone is about to die, you are wasting most of her uptime.

## Rotation Examples: Where the Intro Skill Fits

Different team shapes ask different things of a support. The table below sketches how the Shorekeeper Intro Skill slots into common setups; these patterns reflect player experience rather than a single "correct" answer.

| Team archetype | Who fills the Concerto bar | When Shorekeeper enters | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hypercarry (one main DPS) | Your sub-DPS buffer | After the buffer's Outro, before the main DPS | Chains buffs into the carry without losing uptime |
| Dual DPS | Either damage dealer | Between damage windows | Heals both carries through chip damage |
| Quickswap | Everyone, constantly | On cooldown, whenever Concerto is ready | Keeps the field buff active with minimal downtime |
| Survival-focused | Your tankiest character | Right before a known heavy attack | The instant swap-in doubles as an escape |

### A practical boss-fight loop

1. Open with your sub-DPS and build Concerto while chipping the boss.
2. Swap to Shorekeeper — the Intro Skill fires, healing the team.
3. Immediately drop her Resonance Skill and, if energy allows, her Liberation.
4. Swap out so her Outro buff lands on your main DPS.
5. Burn your damage window, then rebuild Concerto and repeat.

The rhythm matters more than the exact order. Once you internalize that her entry move is the beat that starts each cycle, the rest of the rotation tends to organize itself.

## Common Mistakes and Pro Tips

Most complaints about Shorekeeper being "underwhelming" trace back to a handful of repeatable errors. Here is how to spot and fix them.

| Mistake | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Swapping in with an empty Concerto bar | No entry animation, no heal | Wait for the portrait ring to fill first |
| Leaving her on field too long | Damage window shrinks, energy stalls | Swap out as soon as her Outro is ready |
| Ignoring Concerto on supports | Rotation never reaches Shorekeeper | Prioritize Concerto generation on your sub-DPS |
| Saving her entry for emergencies | Team health slowly bleeds out | Use it proactively, on a rhythm |
| Skipping Intro Skill upgrades | Healing feels weak in late content | Level the node like any other core skill |

Pro tips worth adopting early:

- **Bank her entry for telegraphed attacks.** The brief swap-in window is one of the cheapest dodges in the game.
- **Pair the Intro Skill with a reposition.** Bring her in near the boss so your follow-up DPS does not have to sprint back into range.
- **Count your swaps.** In a well-tuned team, she should be entering the field roughly once per rotation cycle — if it is less, your Concerto generation needs work.

If you are struggling to trigger it, look at your sub-DPS rotation before you look at her gear.

## FAQ

**What triggers the Shorekeeper Intro Skill?**

It fires automatically when you swap Shorekeeper onto the field while the outgoing character has a full Concerto bar. If that bar is not full, the swap is a plain switch and no Intro Skill occurs. There is no manual input involved — the trigger is entirely about resource management on the character you are leaving.

**Is the Shorekeeper Intro Skill a heal or a damage skill?**

It is both. She deals Spectro damage as she lands and restores health to the team. The damage is secondary to the healing in most builds, but it means her entry is never a wasted turn. Exact scaling should be confirmed in the in-game skill description.

**How often can I use the Shorekeeper Intro Skill in one rotation?**

In a well-built team, expect it roughly once per rotation cycle — the same rhythm as any other swap-in move. The limiting factor is almost always Concerto generation on your other characters, not a cooldown on Shorekeeper herself.

**Do I need to level the Intro Skill node separately?**

Yes. It is its own upgrade track, and skipping it is one of the most common reasons players feel her healing falls off in harder content. Treat it as a core skill rather than an afterthought, and upgrade it alongside her Resonance Skill and Liberation.
