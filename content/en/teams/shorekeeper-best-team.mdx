Building the Shorekeeper best team is one of the fastest ways to raise your damage ceiling in Wuthering Waves. She is the rare support who does three jobs at once: she heals, she amplifies crit, and she keeps your main damage dealer alive long enough to finish a full rotation. Get the Shorekeeper best team right, and every other investment you have made in your roster suddenly performs better.

This guide breaks down what she actually contributes, which partners make the most of her kit, and how to sequence a rotation so her buffs land on your biggest hits instead of being wasted on a sub-DPS.

## Why Shorekeeper Changes How You Build a Team

Most teams in Wuthering Waves are built around a simple three-slot formula: one main DPS, one sub-DPS or buffer, and one sustain unit. Shorekeeper compresses two of those roles into a single slot, which is why she shows up in so many endgame compositions.

That compression matters because it frees the third slot for a second damage dealer instead of a pure healer. In practice, a team running Shorekeeper often deals noticeably more total damage than the same team running a dedicated healer, simply because one more character is contributing to the damage window.

She is also a Spectro Rectifier user, so she scales with Energy Regen rather than raw attack. That single stat choice shapes every build decision that follows.

## What Shorekeeper Actually Brings to the Table

Before picking partners, it helps to know exactly what you are shopping for. Her value is not "big heals" — it is uptime on buffs that are difficult to get anywhere else.

| Contribution | What It Means for the Team | Who Benefits Most |
|---|---|---|
| Team-wide healing | Sustains the whole party without eating field time | Glass-cannon DPS units |
| Crit Rate and Crit DMG amplification | Raises the value of every crit substat you own | Any main DPS with a burst window |
| A persistent buff field | Keeps buffs active while you swap and cycle | Quickswap and hypercarry teams |
| Energy Regen scaling | Her buffs improve as you stack one stat | Players who can farm ER echoes |
| Reliable Outro Skill buff | Hands a damage boost to whoever swaps in next | Main DPS units with long animations |

The crit amplification is the headline. Crit is the hardest stat to stack in most action RPGs, and a support who hands it out to the entire party changes how aggressively you can build your DPS.

## The Three-Slot Formula for a Shorekeeper Best Team

Nearly every successful composition follows the same skeleton. The names change, the logic does not.

| Slot | Job Description | Typical Candidates |
|---|---|---|
| Slot 1 — Main DPS | Deals the overwhelming majority of damage inside a burst window | Jinhsi, Jiyan, Camellya, Changli, Xiangli Yao, Carlotta |
| Slot 2 — Amplifier | Applies elemental resistance shred, attack buffs, or coordinated attacks | Zhezhi, Yinlin, Mortefi, Sanhua |
| Slot 3 — Shorekeeper | Heals, buffs crit, and anchors the rotation | Shorekeeper |

The key insight is that Slot 2 should complement Slot 1's damage type, while Shorekeeper is element-agnostic. She fits into Glacio, Fusion, Havoc, Electro, Aero, and Spectro teams equally well, which is why she is often called the most flexible support in the game.

If you are short on characters, prioritize Slot 2 over Slot 1. A slightly weaker DPS with a perfect amplifier will outperform a strong DPS with no support.

### Quickswap Variants

If you enjoy fast, swap-heavy play, Shorekeeper works beautifully in two-DPS quickswap teams. You drop her field, then alternate between two damage dealers so that both benefit from the crit window. The tradeoff is that you lose a dedicated amplifier, so this style rewards high-investment characters.

## Rotation Guide: Turning Her Kit Into Damage

A good Shorekeeper best team still fails if the rotation is sloppy. The goal is simple: have her buff field active before your main DPS commits to their biggest attack.

| Step | Action | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open with your amplifier | Apply debuffs and buffs early so they overlap the damage window |
| 2 | Swap to Shorekeeper and use her Skill | Heal the team and begin building Concerto energy |
| 3 | Trigger her Liberation | Place the persistent buff field before the DPS commits |
| 4 | Use her Outro Skill into your main DPS | Hand the damage boost directly to your carry |
| 5 | Burn the main DPS burst window | Spend every cooldown inside the buff field |
| 6 | Cycle back to the amplifier | Reset the loop before buffs expire |

The single most common error is using Shorekeeper's Liberation too early, then letting the field expire while you are still setting up. Watch the field timer and reapply it before your carry's second big hit.

## Building Shorekeeper So Her Team Buffs Peak

Her build is unusual because it is almost entirely about one stat. Community testing commonly points to an Energy Regen target in the region of 250%, the point at which her crit buffs are reported to reach their top tier. Everything else is secondary.

| Build Element | Priority | Reasoning |

A Bell-Borne Geochelone echo is a solid pick if your team is struggling to survive, since the shield buys extra time inside the damage window.

## Common Mistakes in Shorekeeper Best Team Builds

Even experienced players fall into the same traps. Here are the ones that cost the most damage.

- **Running two sustain units.** Pairing Shorekeeper with another healer wastes a slot that could hold an amplifier. She is enough on her own.
- **Chasing attack stats.** Her buffs scale with Energy Regen, not attack. Attack rolls on her echoes do almost nothing for the team.
- **Ignoring the Outro Skill.** The buff transfer to the incoming character is a large part of her value. Skipping it is leaving damage on the table.
- **Letting the field drop.** Reapply her Liberation before your carry's second rotation, not after.
- **Building her as a damage dealer.** Her personal damage is modest. Invest in uptime instead.
- **Forgetting Concerto energy.** If her Concerto is not ready, the rotation stalls. Practice the loop until it feels automatic.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Shorekeeper good with every main DPS?**
Yes — she is element-agnostic, so she fits any team that has room for a sustain unit. That flexibility is exactly why the Shorekeeper best team conversation usually starts with "who is my DPS?" rather than "what element is she?"

**Who is the single best partner for Shorekeeper?**
There is no universal answer, but Zhezhi, Yinlin, Mortefi, and Sanhua are the amplifiers most often paired with her. Match the amplifier to your main DPS's damage type and you will rarely go wrong.

**What Energy Regen should I aim for?**
Community reports generally point to roughly 250% as the threshold where her crit buffs reach maximum value. Below that, the team still functions — you simply get a smaller damage boost.

**Can Shorekeeper replace Verina?**
In most compositions, yes. Verina offers stronger raw healing and a universal damage buff, while Shorekeeper offers crit amplification and better buff persistence through swaps. For timed endgame content, most players lean toward Shorekeeper.

**Does she need her signature weapon?**
No. Any Rectifier with a strong Energy Regen substat will get you close, though her dedicated weapon smooths out the stat requirements considerably.
