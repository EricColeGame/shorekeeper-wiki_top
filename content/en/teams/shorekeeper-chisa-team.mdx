## Why the Shorekeeper Chisa Team Deserves a Slot in Your Roster

A Shorekeeper Chisa team solves the two hardest problems in Wuthering Waves team building at the same time: keeping your damage dealer alive and squeezing every point of damage out of a short on-field window. Shorekeeper covers healing, crowd comfort, and stat amplification, while Chisa supplies the burst damage that turns a safe team into a fast one. If you have been running a pure survival support with a glass-cannon DPS and wondering why your clear times stall, this pairing is the fix.

The appeal of a Shorekeeper Chisa team is that neither half is dead weight. Shorekeeper's kit is built around converting Energy Regen into crit buffs for whoever she hands off to, which means her value scales with your investment instead of capping out early. Chisa, meanwhile, rewards players who learn her timing windows and punish players who panic-swap. Put them together with the right third slot and you get a squad that clears endgame content without needing perfect dodges on every attack.

This guide breaks down roles, third-slot options, rotation order, echo priorities, and the small habits that separate a smooth Shorekeeper Chisa team from a messy one. Kit details shift with balance patches, so always double-check in-game tooltips and the [official Wuthering Waves site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/) before committing resources.

## Shorekeeper's Role: The Engine Behind the Team

Shorekeeper is a Spectro support who heals through her field effect and then hands off a bundle of offensive buffs when she leaves the field. Her Outro Skill is the reason she is considered a premium support rather than a budget healer: it converts Energy Regen into crit-rate and crit-damage bonuses for the incoming Resonator. That conversion is what makes Energy Regen her single most important stat — not because she needs to survive, but because her buffs literally scale with it.

In practice, this means Shorekeeper wants to be on the field just long enough to drop her healing field, build her Forte gauge, and exit. She is not a damage dealer, and treating her like one is the fastest way to lose DPS. Her job is to make the next eight to twelve seconds of Chisa's field time dramatically stronger.

| Shorekeeper Contribution | What It Does for Chisa | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Healing field from her Liberation | Lets Chisa stay aggressive instead of dodging out of damage windows | High |
| Outro crit buffs | Raises both crit rate and crit damage for Chisa's burst window | Critical |
| Energy Regen scaling | Turns a defensive stat into team-wide offense | Critical |
| Grouping and stagger utility | Keeps small enemies inside Chisa's damage area | Situational |

The practical takeaway: build Shorekeeper like a battery, not a bruiser. Energy Regen on every echo and substat you can spare, then Healing Bonus where it fits.

## Chisa's Role: The Damage Half of the Equation

Chisa is the reason this composition exists. She is a damage-focused Resonator whose kit rewards committed on-field time — the longer you let her cycle through her own abilities without interruption, the more her output compounds. That makes her a natural partner for Shorekeeper, because Shorekeeper's buffs are applied to the incoming Resonator and persist through Chisa's entire damage window.

Two habits matter most when piloting Chisa inside a Shorekeeper Chisa team:

- **Do not swap out mid-combo.** Every early swap throws away buffed damage frames you already paid for with Shorekeeper's field time.
- **Line up her biggest hits with the buff window.** If your rotation drifts, you will land your strongest attacks after the crit buffs expire, which is the single most common reason players report disappointing clear times.

Community reports consistently describe Chisa as a character with a slightly steeper learning curve than her animations suggest — she feels clunky for the first few hours and then clicks once the rotation becomes muscle memory. Player experience also suggests that Chisa benefits more from crit consistency than from stacking raw attack, which is exactly what Shorekeeper provides.

## Best Third Slots for a Shorekeeper Chisa Team

The third slot is where you tune the team to the content. You generally want a sub-DPS or a second buffer who can contribute damage during Shorekeeper's downtime rather than a second healer.

| Third-Slot Type | What They Add | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Off-field sub-DPS | Damage while Chisa is off the field and faster energy generation | Most endgame fights, Tower of Adversity floors |
| Second buffer or debuffer | Extra amplification stacked on top of Shorekeeper's crit buffs | Short, burst-heavy fights where you want one huge window |
| Elemental enabler | Applies a status that sets up Chisa's damage type | Bosses with element-specific resistances |
| Second healer or shielder | Safety net and forgiveness for missed dodges | Learning Chisa's timing, co-op, high-pressure fights |

A practical rule from the community: if your clear times are slow, add damage; if you are dying, you are probably mistiming Chisa's windows rather than lacking a healer. Most players who run a Shorekeeper Chisa team find that one support plus one sub-DPS produces faster clears than two supports.

When in doubt, pick a third slot that generates Concerto quickly. Faster Concerto means faster Outro Skills, which means Shorekeeper's buffs come back online sooner and Chisa spends more of the fight buffed.

## Rotation Blueprint and Combat Flow

Rotation order matters more than raw stats in a Shorekeeper Chisa team. The goal is simple: apply buffs, then spend them efficiently, then reset.

| Step | Action | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open with your third slot's setup ability | Applies debuffs or generates Concerto before buffs land |
| 2 | Swap to Shorekeeper and activate her field | Starts healing and begins building her Forte gauge |
| 3 | Complete Shorekeeper's Forte and trigger her Outro | Passes crit buffs to the next Resonator |
| 4 | Swap to Chisa and run her full damage combo | Spends the buff window on your highest-damage abilities |
| 5 | Use Chisa's Outro to hand off, then reset | Restarts the loop with buffs refreshed |

Three details separate clean rotations from sloppy ones:

1. **Do not waste Shorekeeper's field.** If you drop it and then spend four seconds dodging, the healing and buff timing both drift.
2. **Watch your Concerto bars, not the health bars.** Energy management is the real skill check.
3. **Reset early if a fight goes sideways.** A half-finished rotation is worse than a fresh one.

Player experience suggests that recording one clear and rewatching it reveals more about rotation problems than another hour of grinding. You will usually spot two or three wasted seconds immediately.

## Build Priorities: Echoes, Stats, and Weapons

Build the two halves differently. Shorekeeper wants Energy Regen above all else; Chisa wants crit and damage.

| Build Slot | Shorekeeper | Chisa |
|---|---|---|
| Primary stat focus | Energy Regen, then Healing Bonus | Crit Rate and Crit DMG |
| Echo set goal | Energy Regen and team buff effects | Damage-focused set matching her element |
| Substats to chase | Energy Regen on every piece | Crit rolls, then attack |
| Weapon priority | Energy Regen passive or team buff | Highest base attack or crit passive |
| Common mistake | Stacking HP for "tankiness" | Chasing attack over crit consistency |

For Shorekeeper, the target is straightforward: hit the Energy Regen threshold her Outro scaling demands, then stop and invest elsewhere. Overshooting Energy Regen past the cap is wasted stats that could have gone into survivability or healing.

For Chisa, prioritize a crit ratio that lets her biggest hits land consistently. Because Shorekeeper already contributes crit stats, you can often shift one or two echo substats away from crit rate and into crit damage — but only once your rotation is stable enough to keep the buff active during her damage window.

## Common Mistakes and Pro Tips

- **Swapping Chisa out too early.** Buffed frames are the most expensive resource in the team.
- **Ignoring Concerto management.** If your third slot never generates energy, Shorekeeper's buffs stay offline.
- **Building Shorekeeper as a damage dealer.** Her scaling is not there, and you lose buff uptime.
- **Copying a showcase rotation from a different team.** Showcase videos often assume perfect enemy behavior and specific investment levels.
- **Neglecting dodge timing on Chisa.** Her damage windows are long enough that a single interruption can cost a full rotation.

A useful habit: after every failed endgame attempt, ask whether you died or whether you ran out of time. Those two problems have completely different fixes in a Shorekeeper Chisa team — one is a rotation issue, the other is an investment issue.

## FAQ

**Is a Shorekeeper Chisa team good for beginners?**
Yes, with a caveat. Shorekeeper's healing makes the team forgiving, and Chisa's damage ceiling is high, but the rotation takes practice. Start with a defensive third slot, then swap to a sub-DPS once you can survive without the safety net.

**What Energy Regen should Shorekeeper have in a Shorekeeper Chisa team?**
Enough to hit the cap on her Outro Skill's crit buff scaling. Check the current in-game tooltip for the exact threshold, since balance patches can change it, and stop investing in Energy Regen once you reach it.

**Who should be the third member of a Shorekeeper Chisa team?**
An off-field sub-DPS or a second buffer is the safest general answer. Choose an elemental enabler only when a specific boss resists Chisa's damage type.

**Can I run two healers with Chisa instead?**
You can, but you will usually clear slower. A Shorekeeper Chisa team already has strong survivability, so a second healer trades damage for safety you probably do not need.
