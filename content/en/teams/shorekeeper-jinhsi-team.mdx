## Why the Shorekeeper Jinhsi Team Works So Well

If you want Jinhsi's Incandescence-charged nuke to hit its ceiling, the **Shorekeeper Jinhsi team** is the fastest route there. Shorekeeper layers healing, a Crit. Rate boost, and a Crit. DMG buff that line up with Jinhsi's damage window — and no other support in Wuthering Waves does it quite as cleanly. That's why the **Shorekeeper Jinhsi team** sits near the top of nearly every Spectro hypercarry tier list.

This guide breaks down the composition, the best third-slot picks, build priorities, and the exact rotation order that keeps Jinhsi's damage window covered.

Jinhsi doesn't deal damage the way most main DPS characters do. She spends her field time building **Incandescence** while teammates attack, then converts that resource into one enormous Resonance Skill hit. Her kit rewards two things: allies who attack constantly, and buffs that are live during a very short window.

Shorekeeper covers the second half of that equation. Her Stellarealm heals the active character and grants a Crit. Rate boost, while her Energy Regen feeds an additional Crit. DMG contribution. Those are exactly the stats Jinhsi's nuke wants.

There's a structural bonus too: Wuthering Waves has no elemental reaction system, so pairing two Spectro characters costs you nothing. You aren't wasting an element — you're stacking the game's premier support onto its premier Spectro damage dealer.

| Jinhsi's requirement | What Shorekeeper provides | Practical effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crit. Rate on the big hit | Crit. Rate buff inside the Stellarealm | Fewer missed crits on the nuke |
| Crit. DMG scaling | Crit. DMG bonus that scales with her Energy Regen | Higher damage ceiling |
| Survival during long combos | Continuous healing inside the field | Fewer interrupted rotations |
| Minimal support field time | Compact, low-maintenance kit | More seconds on Jinhsi |

## Core Composition and Role Breakdown

The standard build is a hypercarry shell: one main DPS, one off-field sub-DPS, and one support-healer. Jinhsi owns the damage windows, Shorekeeper owns the buffs and healing, and the third slot exists to feed Incandescence as quickly as possible.

| Slot | Character | Primary job | Field time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main DPS | Jinhsi | Build Incandescence, then fire the enhanced Resonance Skill | High, in bursts |
| Support / Healer | Shorekeeper | Heal, buff Crit. Rate and Crit. DMG, anchor the rotation | Low |
| Sub-DPS | Zhezhi | Coordinated attacks that feed Incandescence | Medium |

Zhezhi is the default third pick because her coordinated attacks keep triggering even while she's off the field. More triggers mean faster Incandescence stacks, which means Jinhsi reaches her damage window sooner. Zhezhi can also carry a Moonlit Clouds set to pass an ATK buff to Jinhsi through her Outro Skill — a small detail that compounds over a long fight.

If you don't own Shorekeeper yet, Verina is the usual substitute. She heals and buffs, but she doesn't bring the Crit. DMG scaling that defines the Shorekeeper Jinhsi team, so expect a noticeable drop in Jinhsi's ceiling. It's also worth checking the [official Wuthering Waves site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/en/) for current banner schedules before you commit pulls to either character.

## Best Third-Slot Options for the Shorekeeper Jinhsi Team

The support slot is settled. The interesting decision is who joins Jinhsi and Shorekeeper.

| Pick | Rarity | How it helps | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zhezhi | 5-star | Coordinated attacks plus Moonlit Clouds ATK transfer | Needs her own build investment |
| Yinlin | 5-star | Reliable off-field coordinated damage | Slightly slower stack generation |
| Yuanwu | 4-star | Cheap, consistent coordinated attacks | Low personal damage |
| Changli | 5-star | Adds a second damage dealer for dual-DPS play | Splits field time with Jinhsi |
| Sanhua | 4-star | Fast Concerto generation and quick swaps | Her buff targets basic attacks, not Jinhsi's skill nuke |

Two notes from player experience. First, Yuanwu is widely reported as the best budget option — he's inexpensive to build, and his field generates the hits Jinhsi needs to stack. Second, don't assume any fast-swapping character fits. Jinhsi's damage comes from her Resonance Skill, so buffs that only affect basic attacks do very little for her, no matter how quickly the character cycles in and out.

## Build and Gear Priorities

Shorekeeper is the easiest character on the team to build, and that's by design. She doesn't need Crit stats of her own. Community guides generally agree that Energy Regen is her single most important substat, since it improves both her healing uptime and the Crit. DMG she contributes. Many players push her Energy Regen very high — often past 250% — specifically to maximize that buff.

Jinhsi, by contrast, wants a conventional DPS spread: Crit. Rate, Crit. DMG, ATK, and Spectro DMG bonus, plus enough Energy Regen to keep her Liberation available on cooldown.

| Character | Weapon priority | Echo set | Stat priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jinhsi | Signature 5-star Broadblade, then a standard 5-star option | 5-piece Spectro DMG set (Celestial Light) | Crit. Rate / Crit. DMG / ATK / Spectro DMG |
| Shorekeeper | Signature Rectifier, or a 4-star with an Energy Regen passive (Variation) | 5-piece healing set (Rejuvenating Glow) | Energy Regen / HP / healing bonus |
| Zhezhi | Signature Rectifier, or any Energy Regen Rectifier | 5-piece Moonlit Clouds | Energy Regen / ATK / Crit |

| Echo cost | Jinhsi | Shorekeeper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4-cost | Spectro DMG or Crit. DMG | Healing bonus or HP |
| 3-cost | Spectro DMG | Energy Regen |
| 1-cost | ATK | HP |

For Shorekeeper's 4-cost Echo, defensive or team-oriented options are the norm. A support Echo that shields or buffs the party usually beats anything raising her personal damage — she simply isn't on the field long enough for her own numbers to matter.

## Rotation Guide: How to Sequence the Shorekeeper Jinhsi Team

The rotation is shorter than it looks. The entire goal is to have Shorekeeper's field and buffs active at the exact moment Jinhsi fires her enhanced Resonance Skill.

| Step | Action | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open on Shorekeeper, land basic attacks to charge Concerto, then trigger her Resonance Liberation | Plant the Stellarealm before anyone takes damage |
| 2 | Swap to Zhezhi, deploy her coordinated-attack summon, then use her Liberation | Start feeding Incandescence |
| 3 | Outro from Zhezhi into Jinhsi | Pass the Moonlit Clouds ATK buff to Jinhsi |
| 4 | On Jinhsi, chain basic attacks and skill casts until Incandescence is full | Reach the 50-stack cap |
| 5 | Fire the enhanced Resonance Skill, then her Liberation | Land the nuke inside the buff window |
| 6 | Swap back to Shorekeeper and refresh the Stellarealm | Reset for the next loop |

A few habits separate a clean clear from a messy one:

- **Never Outro into the wrong character.** Shorekeeper's buff should land on Jinhsi right before her damage window, not on the sub-DPS.
- **Refresh early, not late.** If the Stellarealm expires mid-nuke, you lose Crit. Rate and Crit. DMG on the biggest hit of the fight.
- **Don't fire the nuke below max stacks.** Incandescence caps at 50; dumping it at 30 wastes a full rotation.
- **Watch Concerto energy.** Shorekeeper can't pass her Outro buff without a full Concerto bar, so don't burn her skill casts for nothing.

| Mistake | Why it costs you | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Letting the Stellarealm drop before the nuke | Loses Crit. buffs on the largest hit | Refresh Shorekeeper's field one step earlier |
| Building Crit on Shorekeeper | Wasted substats on a character who rarely attacks | Stack Energy Regen and HP instead |
| Running two healers | Thins out your total damage | Use Shorekeeper or Verina, never both |
| Nuking at low Incandescence | Wastes the entire rotation | Wait for the 50-stack cap |

## FAQ

**Is Shorekeeper better than Verina for Jinhsi?**
For raw damage, yes. The Shorekeeper Jinhsi team produces higher numbers because Shorekeeper contributes Crit. Rate and scaling Crit. DMG, while Verina leans toward ATK and healing. If you own both, Shorekeeper is the pick.

**Who is the best third teammate?**
Zhezhi, for most accounts. Her coordinated attacks generate Incandescence faster than any alternative, and she can hold Moonlit Clouds to pass an ATK buff. Yinlin is a close second, and Yuanwu is the strongest budget choice.

**How much Energy Regen does Shorekeeper need?**
Community reports consistently point to very high Energy Regen — commonly 250% or more — because her Crit. DMG contribution scales with it. Treat it as your top substat priority and don't chase Crit on her.

**Can the team work without Zhezhi?**
Yes. Any character with frequent off-field attacks can fill the slot. Yuanwu and Yinlin are the most common substitutes, and both keep Incandescence flowing well enough to clear endgame content comfortably.
