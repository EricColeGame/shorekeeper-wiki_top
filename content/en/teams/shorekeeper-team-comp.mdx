## Why Shorekeeper Belongs on Almost Every Roster

Shorekeeper is the rare Wuthering Waves support who heals your team and raises its damage ceiling at the same time. If you are still treating her as a panic-button healer, your Shorekeeper team comp is leaving a huge amount of damage on the table, because her Crit. Rate and Crit. DMG buffs scale off a stat most players ignore. She also frees up a slot: as a solo sustain she replaces both a healer and a buffer, which is why so many Shorekeeper team comps run three damage dealers instead of the classic two-plus-healer setup.

This guide covers the archetypes that actually work, the pairings that produce the cleanest rotations, the build targets you need to hit, and the budget swaps to use when a key five-star is missing. Numbers below reflect community reports and player experience rather than official patch notes, so treat them as tested guidelines rather than hard rules.

## What Shorekeeper Actually Contributes

Understanding the kit is the difference between a Shorekeeper team comp that carries and one that just survives. Her value is concentrated in three places: healing throughput, a Crit. buff she passes forward through her Outro Skill, and an unusually small field-time footprint.

| Contribution | How it works | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Team healing | Her Intro Skill and Resonance Liberation restore HP to the active resonator and the team over time | Keeps a three-damage-dealer setup alive without a second healer |
| Crit. Rate buff | Passed to the incoming resonator through her Outro Skill | Lifts every damage dealer regardless of element |
| Crit. DMG buff | Scales upward with her Energy Regen, capping around 250% ER | Multiplies burst windows from Jinhsi, Camellya, Carlotta and similar carries |
| Short field time | Her kit is designed to be fired off and swapped out | Leaves the majority of the fight to your main DPS |
| Concerto generation | Skill plus enhanced attacks fill her Outro quickly | Rotations stay smooth even in fast quick-swap teams |

### Energy Regen is the stat that decides everything

Shorekeeper is a buff amplifier first and a healer second. Her Crit. buffs grow as her Energy Regen climbs, and player experience consistently points to roughly 250% Energy Regen as the point where those buffs effectively cap out. Below that, you are running an expensive healer with a mediocre buff.

Healing, meanwhile, scales with HP. That creates a mild tension: you want ER everywhere, then pour the leftovers into HP% and flat HP. Crit stats are close to worthless on her, because she is not the one dealing damage.

## Shorekeeper Build Checklist

Weapons, echoes, and stats matter more on Shorekeeper than on most supports, because her buffs are gated behind a stat threshold.

| Weapon | Rarity | Why players pick it |
|---|---|---|
| Stellar Symphony | 5★ | Signature option: Energy Regen substat plus a team-wide ATK buff |
| Variation | 4★ | The cheapest reliable way to reach Energy Regen targets |
| Any high-ER Rectifier | 4★ | Works as filler; you lose the ATK buff, not the Crit. buffs |

| Echo Set | Effect | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Rejuvenating Glow (5-piece) | Healing bonus plus a team ATK buff after healing | Default pick for almost every team |
| Moonlit Clouds (5-piece) | ATK buff passed to the next resonator after Outro | Alternative when your DPS scales hard on ATK |
| 2-piece hybrids | Mixed healing and Energy Regen bonuses | Early-game stopgap while farming |

| Stat | Target | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Energy Regen | ~250% | Caps her Crit. buffs |
| HP% | As high as possible | Directly scales healing output |
| 4-cost main stat | Healing Bonus or HP% | Healing Bonus for safety, HP% for raw scaling |
| 3-cost main stats | Energy Regen ×2 | Hitting the ER target comes first |
| 1-cost main stats | HP% ×2 | Cheapest source of healing throughput |
| Substats | ER > HP% > flat HP | Do not chase Crit on a support |

The 4-cost echo choice is worth a note. Community reports favor an echo that grants the team an ATK buff after healing, since Shorekeeper heals constantly and keeps the buff permanently refreshed. A defensive shield echo is the safer alternative if your rotations are still shaky.

## Rotation: Making the Team Feel Smooth

Shorekeeper's rotation is short by design. The goal is to enter, deploy everything, and leave before your carry's damage window opens.

| Step | Action | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Swap in with her Intro Skill | Heals the team and starts building Concerto |
| 2 | Resonance Skill | Deploys her healing field and begins Forte stacks |
| 3 | Enhanced basic attacks | Finishes her Forte Circuit and fills Concerto |
| 4 | Resonance Liberation | Extends healing uptime across the whole team |
| 5 | Outro Skill into your DPS | Hands over the Crit. Rate and Crit. DMG buffs |
| 6 | DPS burst window | Spend every buff before it expires |
| 7 | Reset | Rebuild Concerto on Shorekeeper and repeat |

A few practical tips from player experience:

- Fire her Ultimate before the Outro, never after — the buffs should already be active when your DPS starts swinging.
- Do not linger on Shorekeeper to top off HP. Her healing follows the active character, so it keeps working after you swap.
- Align her Outro with your carry's biggest cooldown. A Crit. buff spent on basic attacks is a wasted rotation.
- In quick-swap teams, re-enter Shorekeeper only when her Concerto is full, not on every cooldown.

## Common Mistakes That Wreck a Shorekeeper Team Comp

- **Stacking HP and ignoring Energy Regen.** A 300% HP Shorekeeper with 180% ER heals well and buffs badly. ER first, always.
- **Treating her as an on-field healer.** She is a setup unit. Every extra second she spends attacking is a second your carry is not.
- **Running a second healer "for safety."** Shorekeeper's whole appeal is that she opens a slot for more damage.
- **Using her Outro before her Ultimate.** The buff window and healing window should overlap.
- **Chasing Crit substats.** They do nothing for her kit and slow down your ER farming.
- **Forgetting to refresh the field.** Long fights need a second rotation; plan for it rather than reacting to low HP.

## FAQ

**What is the best Shorekeeper team comp for most players?**
A hypercarry setup built around one strong main DPS, one amplifier, and Shorekeeper is the most reliable Shorekeeper team comp. It is easy to pilot, forgiving of small rotation errors, and scales well as you improve your gear.

**How much Energy Regen does Shorekeeper need?**
Community testing puts the effective cap around 250% Energy Regen. Aim for at least 230% before you invest in HP, and treat anything above 250% as a comfort buffer rather than a damage gain.

**Can Shorekeeper be the only healer on a team?**
Yes. Her Intro Skill, Resonance Skill field, and Ultimate together provide enough sustain for most endgame content. That is exactly why she frees up a slot for a third damage dealer.

**Is Shorekeeper worth pulling if I already have Verina?**
They solve different problems. Verina buffs ATK and deepens your damage; Shorekeeper buffs Crit. Rate and Crit. DMG and usually fits teams built around burst windows. If you main a Crit-hungry carry, the upgrade is noticeable.
