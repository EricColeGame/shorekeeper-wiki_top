Few convenes in Wuthering Waves hold their value as long as the Shorekeeper banner. She is a Spectro support who heals and amplifies damage, which means she slots into almost every endgame team you can build. If you are deciding whether to spend your Astrite on the Shorekeeper banner or save for the next new character, this guide covers the pity math, the real cost of a guarantee, her team value, and how to plan for a rerun.

## Shorekeeper Banner Basics: Pity, Rates, and Guarantees

Shorekeeper is a 5-star Spectro character who wields a Rectifier, introduced during the game's 1.3 update cycle. Her featured convene follows the same rules as every other limited character banner in the game, which is good news: the mechanics are predictable, and pity carries over between limited character banners.

That last point matters more than anything else on this page. If you pulled 40 times on the previous featured banner and lost your 50/50, all of that progress and your guarantee follow you into the Shorekeeper banner.

| Mechanic | How It Works |
|---|---|
| Banner type | Featured Character Convene |
| Base 5-star rate | 0.8% per pull |
| Hard pity | 80 pulls |
| Soft pity | Rates climb noticeably from roughly pull 65 onward |
| Featured guarantee | 50/50 on your first 5-star; guaranteed featured if you lose |
| 4-star guarantee | At least one 4-star or higher every 10 pulls |
| Pull currency | Radiant Tide (160 Astrite each) |
| Pity carryover | Yes — carries between limited character banners |

The practical takeaway: never assume a "fresh" banner resets your progress. Check your history in-game before you spend, because a partially built pity count can turn an expensive pull session into a cheap one.

## What It Actually Costs to Guarantee Shorekeeper

Most players underestimate how much a worst-case scenario costs. Below is the Astrite math for the common situations players find themselves in when a Shorekeeper banner goes live.

| Your Situation | Pulls Needed (Worst Case) | Astrite Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Zero pity, no guarantee | 160 (lose 50/50, then hit pity again) | 25,600 |
| Zero pity, guaranteed featured | 80 | 12,800 |
| Sitting at soft pity (about 65 pulls in) | 15–20 | 2,400–3,200 |
| 50/50 win at hard pity | 80 | 12,800 |
| 40 pulls in, no guarantee | 120 | 19,200 |

A few habits make that number far less intimidating:

- **Track your pity in a notes app.** Write down your pull count after every session so you never guess.
- **Convert everything to pulls, not Astrite.** Thinking in "pulls" makes it obvious whether a new event is worth 5 or 15 pulls.
- **Use the monthly tide exchange.** The Oscillated Coral shop refreshes discounted tides each month — grab them before they expire.
- **Don't convert Astrite into standard pulls.** Lustrous Tide convenes do not build pity toward a limited banner.

If you are starting from zero with no guarantee, plan for roughly 25,600 Astrite. That is the honest ceiling, and budgeting for it prevents the frustration of a last-minute top-up.

## Should You Pull? Shorekeeper's Team Value Explained

Shorekeeper is not a niche pick. She is a generalist support, which means the Shorekeeper banner is one of the safest investments a returning or mid-game player can make. Her kit centers on healing and a field effect that boosts her team's critical stats, and community build guides consistently report that those buffs scale with her Energy Regeneration.

How does she compare to the game's other headline healer? Here is the community consensus.

| Aspect | Shorekeeper | Verina |
|---|---|---|
| Element | Spectro | Spectro |
| Weapon | Rectifier | Rectifier |
| Primary role | Healer and crit buffer | Healer and ATK buffer |
| Signature effect | Field that raises team crit stats | Team-wide ATK boost |
| Build demand | Higher Energy Regen investment | Easier to build early |
| Best home | Crit-reliant hypercarry teams | Flexible generalist teams |

Neither is "wrong." Verina remains a comfortable, low-investment option, while Shorekeeper raises the ceiling of teams that lean on critical hits.

She fits a wide range of compositions:

| Team Archetype | Typical Main DPS | Why Shorekeeper Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Spectro hypercarry | Jinhsi | Crit buffs stack with Spectro damage windows |
| Havoc hypercarry | Camellya | Extends burst windows with healing uptime |
| Fusion quickswap | Changli, Encore | Keeps fragile DPS alive during swaps |
| Generalist endgame | Any 5-star DPS | Universal support slot in Tower of Adversity |

If you already own two or three well-built DPS characters but only one healer, the Shorekeeper banner solves your biggest account problem. If you are brand new with an empty roster, pulling a limited DPS first is usually the smarter order of operations — supports amplify damage that does not exist yet.

Getting Shorekeeper is step one. The next question is where your remaining resources should go, and the answer is almost always the same.

| Priority | Investment | Why It Ranks Here |

| 3 | Early sequences | Added comfort and buff consistency |

The weapon convene in Wuthering Waves is notably friendlier than the character convene: the featured 5-star weapon is guaranteed within 80 pulls with no 50/50 to lose. That makes Stellar Symphony a predictable purchase rather than a gamble, though it is never mandatory — several free and 4-star Rectifiers can carry her in most content.

For her build, community reports converge on a healing-focused echo set with generous Energy Regeneration. Treat the numbers below as community targets rather than official requirements.

| Stat | Community Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| HP | High priority | Improves healing output |

Upgrade her Forte and Resonance circuits before her basic attack tree. Her damage is negligible; her uptime is everything.

## Shorekeeper Rerun Watch: Planning Your Next Chance

Missed the Shorekeeper banner? Limited characters in Wuthering Waves do return. Kuro Games has cycled reruns alongside new releases, and popular supports tend to come back sooner than niche DPS units because they serve a broader audience. Community reports suggest that support reruns are often scheduled next to new DPS debuts, since new damage dealers need good teammates.

There is no way to confirm a specific rerun date in advance, so plan around the math instead of rumors:

- **Bank 25,600 Astrite** if you want a true worst-case guarantee.
- **Keep your 50/50 guarantee intact.** If you lost a recent 50/50, stop pulling until a banner you truly want arrives.
- **Follow official channels** for schedule announcements rather than datamine chatter. The [official Wuthering Waves news hub](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/en/) posts convene schedules and event calendars directly from the developer.
- **Do not chase 4-stars** on a banner you dislike — it burns pity you will want later.

If a rerun lands while your resources are thin, remember that a partial pity count is not wasted. It carries forward, and every pull you make now shortens the next attempt.

## FAQ

**Does pity carry over between the Shorekeeper banner and other character banners?**
Yes. Limited character convenes share a single pity counter, so pulls made on any featured banner count toward the next one. Your 50/50 guarantee also carries over until it is used.

**How many pulls do I need to guarantee Shorekeeper?**
Worst case, 160 pulls — 80 to hit pity, another 80 if you lose the 50/50 first. That equals 25,600 Astrite. If you already hold a guarantee, the ceiling drops to 80 pulls, or 12,800 Astrite.

**Is Shorekeeper worth pulling if I already have Verina?**
For most players, yes, if their teams rely on critical hits. She raises the damage ceiling of crit-focused compositions, while Verina remains an excellent, easier-to-build alternative. If your roster is still thin on DPS, prioritize a damage dealer first.

**When will the Shorekeeper banner return?**
No official date has been confirmed. Reruns follow the developer's schedule and are announced through official channels, so save your Astrite in advance and watch for convene announcements rather than trusting unverified leaks.
