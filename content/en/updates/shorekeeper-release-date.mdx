Few character debuts in Wuthering Waves reshaped team building as completely as Shorekeeper's, and that is exactly why the Shorekeeper release date is still one of the most searched questions in the community. Before her banner, healers were a safety net you slotted in when a fight ran long. After it, a healer became the buff engine at the center of nearly every endgame squad.

If you are returning to the game, planning a pull, or trying to decide whether a rerun deserves your Asterite, pinning down the Shorekeeper release date gives you the full picture: what shipped alongside her, how she changed the meta, and whether she still holds up today.

## Shorekeeper Release Date at a Glance

Shorekeeper arrived in the first phase of Wuthering Waves Version 1.3, in late September 2024 — roughly four months after the game's global launch. That timing matters. Version 1.3 was the patch where Kuro Games started fleshing out the Black Shores storyline, and Shorekeeper was the headline character carrying it.

Her banner ran for about three weeks, the standard length for a first-phase debut in Wuthering Waves. Because she was a limited 5-star, she did not slide into the standard banner pool when her banner closed — a detail that catches out newer players who assume every character eventually becomes permanent.

## Who Is Shorekeeper in Wuthering Waves?

Shorekeeper is tied to the Black Shores, the research organization that monitors the Lament and the anomalies spreading across Solaris-3. In the story she acts as the custodian of the Black Shores' core — an entity bound to its systems rather than an ordinary Resonator, watching over the Rover long before the two actually meet.

Her appeal is not just mechanical. She arrived with a full narrative arc, a striking astral design, and a personality that sits somewhere between analytical and quietly devoted.

- **Faction:** Black Shores
- **Story role:** Guardian and keeper of the Black Shores' core
- **Tone:** Calm, precise, reserved, deeply loyal
- **Visual identity:** Starlight, deep blues, celestial motifs
- **Narrative hook:** Directly connected to the Rover's missing memories

That framing is a big reason the Shorekeeper release date landed so hard. She was not a filler character dropped into a quiet patch — she was the payoff for a storyline the game had been teasing for months.

## What Made Shorekeeper's Debut So Impactful

She does three jobs at once, and that is the whole reason guides still recommend her: she heals, she buffs, and she smooths out rotations. Most supports in Wuthering Waves pick one of those. Shorekeeper does all three without demanding perfect gear.

| What she contributes | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Sustained healing across a wide area | Keeps teams alive through long boss fights and Tower of Adversity floors |
| Team-wide crit rate and crit damage buffs | Raises the ceiling of every damage dealer on your roster |
| Buffs that scale with Energy Regen | Rewards a stat most supports ignore, keeping her build simple |
| An Outro Skill that passes buffs forward | Lets your main DPS open their combo already buffed |
| A persistent field effect | Keeps buffs active between swaps instead of falling off |

Because her crit buffs scale with Energy Regen rather than her own attack or crit stats, she is one of the cheapest premium supports in the game to build. You are not hunting for perfect crit rolls. You are chasing a single number, and everything else is a bonus.

Player experience backs this up. In community tier discussions since the Shorekeeper release date, she has consistently sat at the top of support rankings alongside Verina — and for many teams, ahead of her.

## Building Shorekeeper: Stats, Weapons, and Echoes

The build is refreshingly narrow. Hit your Energy Regen cap, stack HP, and ignore everything else.

| Stat | Target | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Energy Regen | Around 250% | Caps her team-wide crit buffs |
| HP% | As high as possible | Directly improves her healing output |
| Flat HP | Useful filler | Same purpose as HP% |
| Crit Rate / Crit DMG | Ignore | Her buffs do not scale with her own crit |
| ATK% | Low priority | Contributes very little to her kit |
| Healing Bonus | Optional | Minor gain once Energy Regen is capped |

| Weapon option | Rarity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stellar Symphony | 5-star | Her signature Rectifier; energy-focused and the smoothest fit |
| Variation | 4-star | Free-to-play friendly Energy Regen option |
| Other Energy Regen Rectifiers | 4-star | Fine placeholders until you pull something better |
| Generic high-ATK Rectifiers | Any | Last resort only; the substat does little for her |

| Echo slot | Recommended | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 4-cost | Fallacy of No Return (community favorite) | Team-oriented buff Echo that suits her role |
| 3-cost | Energy Regen main stat | Two of them to reach her cap |
| 1-cost | HP% main stat | Three of them for healing throughput |
| Set | Rejuvenating Glow, 5-piece | The dedicated healing set for supports |

Community reports vary slightly depending on which mode you are tackling. In modes where survival is trivial, some players swap the 4-cost Echo for a more offensive option. In Tower of Adversity, the defensive setup above is the safer default.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Shorekeeper Release Date

**When exactly was the Shorekeeper release date?**
She debuted in the first phase of Wuthering Waves Version 1.3, in late September 2024. That placed her roughly four months after the game's global launch and made her the flagship character of the Black Shores storyline.

**Was Shorekeeper a limited banner character?**
Yes. She is a limited 5-star Spectro Rectifier user and was never added to the standard banner pool. The only way to get her is to pull on her featured banner when it is available.

**Is Shorekeeper still worth pulling today?**
Community consensus since the Shorekeeper release date has remained steady: yes. She is a universal healer and buffer who fits nearly every team composition, and her build is one of the cheapest among premium supports because she only needs Energy Regen and HP.

**How many copies of Shorekeeper do you need?**
None beyond the first. She functions fully at base resonance chain, and additional copies are a luxury rather than a requirement. If you are choosing between a duplicate and a new character, the new character is almost always the better value.
