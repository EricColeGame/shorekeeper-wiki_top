## Why the Shorekeeper Rerun Matters More Than Most

Every gacha game has that one character players regret skipping, and in Wuthering Waves it is almost always Shorekeeper. She is a 5-star Spectro support and healer whose kit boosts her team's Crit Rate and Crit DMG while keeping everyone alive, which makes her one of the most flexible units in the game. That is exactly why a **Shorekeeper rerun** is one of the most anticipated events on the calendar — and why you should start preparing long before a **Shorekeeper rerun** banner actually appears.

Most limited characters are damage dealers, and damage dealers get replaced. Supports who change how your whole account performs tend to stay relevant for years. Shorekeeper sits firmly in that second category, which is why community demand for her return has stayed loud long after her debut banner ended.

This guide walks through how reruns work, what to farm ahead of time, how to build her once you win the 50/50, and whether a Shorekeeper rerun is genuinely worth your Astrite in the current meta.

## How Reruns Work in Wuthering Waves

Wuthering Waves runs its limited banners in timed phases, and each phase typically features more than one character banner running side by side. A rerun simply means a previously featured 5-star returns to that rotation. Kuro Games does not publish a permanent, fixed schedule for returns, so the practical advice is to be ready whenever the announcement lands.

Here is what generally holds true for a Shorekeeper rerun, based on current game rules and player experience:

| Question | What typically happens | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| Is character-banner pity shared? | Yes — pulls on any limited character banner feed the same counter | Progress you built on other banners is not wasted |
| Does a guaranteed featured 5-star carry over? | Yes, it carries to the next character banner | Losing a 50/50 earlier still helps you here |
| Is the weapon banner tied to it? | No, the weapon banner has its own separate pity | Budget for it separately |
| How long does it last? | Usually a few weeks, split across phases | Do not wait until the final day |
| Will she be permanent afterward? | No — she returns only on future reruns | This window may be your best shot for a while |

Rates, pity thresholds, and banner structures can change between updates, so always confirm the current details in the in-game banner info or on the [official Wuthering Waves site and patch notes](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/) before committing your pulls.

## Shorekeeper Rerun Prep Checklist

The single biggest mistake players make around a Shorekeeper rerun is farming everything *after* they pull her. You can pre-farm almost her entire build, so that the moment you unlock her she is immediately usable in endgame content.

| Category | What to gather | Rough target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Character EXP | EXP materials for leveling | Enough for full ascension | Farm from overworld routes and EXP stages |
| Shell Credits | In-game currency | Several million | The silent bottleneck for every build |
| Ascension materials | Element-specific overworld drops plus boss materials | Full six-stage ascension | Check her in-game Ascension preview for exact names |
| Forte materials | Skill and talent upgrade drops | All key skills leveled | Prioritize her buff and heal nodes first |
| Weekly boss drops | Rare weekly materials | Multiple weeks of farming | Start early — these are time-gated |
| Echo EXP and Tuners | Echo leveling resources | One complete 5-piece set | Tuners run out faster than you expect |
| Astrite / Radiant Tides | Pull currency | At least one full pity | Assume the worst case, not the average |

Community reports consistently say the weekly boss materials and Tuners are what hold people back. If you only pre-farm two things before a Shorekeeper rerun, make it those.

### A note on saving pulls

Treat a Shorekeeper rerun as a full-pity project rather than a coin flip. If you go in expecting to win early, you will end up short and frustrated. Track your pity counter, stop pulling on unrelated banners, and convert your Astrite into limited pulls only when you are ready to commit.

## Shorekeeper Rerun Build and Team Cheat Sheet

Shorekeeper is not a complicated unit to build, but she is easy to build *wrong*. She does not want Crit stats of her own — her job is to make everyone else's Crit stats better.

| Slot | Recommendation | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Healer and team buffer | She is a support first, not a damage source |
| Main stats | HP% and Energy Regen | Her healing and buff uptime scale with these |
| Substat priority | Energy Regen first, then HP% | Consistent burst uptime beats raw healing numbers |
| Echo set | Healing-focused set (community favorite: the healing bonus set) | Amplifies her healing and team utility |
| Signature weapon | Stellar Symphony | Adds team-wide Crit DMG on top of her kit |
| Free-to-play weapon | Any Rectifier with Energy Regen or HP% | Perfectly functional for most content |

Player experience suggests that an Energy Regen threshold that lets her burst on rotation matters more than squeezing out extra HP. If your rotations feel slow, you need Energy Regen, not more healing.

### Teams that get the most out of her

| Team style | Core idea | Who else fits |
|---|---|---|
| Spectro hypercarry | Shorekeeper buffs one main DPS to extreme Crit values | A Spectro main DPS plus a sub-DPS |
| Dual-DPS | Her buffs apply broadly, so two damage dealers both benefit | Two main DPS units with a sub-DPS |
| Comfort / sustain | Near-unbreakable survivability for hard endgame modes | Any main DPS plus a shielder or sub-DPS |
| Budget | She alone covers healing and buffing | Any two built damage dealers you own |

A community-favorite pairing is Shorekeeper alongside a Spectro main DPS such as Jinhsi, since both scale off similar team-wide Crit stacking. That said, she slots into almost any composition, which is the whole point of pulling her.

## Should You Pull on the Shorekeeper Rerun?

Not everyone needs her. Here is an honest breakdown by account type:

| Your situation | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| New player without a strong healer | High priority | She solves healing and buffing in one slot |
| Veteran with two fully built healers | Situational | Convenience, not necessity |
| F2P saving for a favorite DPS | Think carefully | Only pull if you can still afford your next target |
| Missing a Crit buffer for endgame modes | High priority | Her buffs lift every team you own |
| Collector / character-focused | Personal choice | Value depends on how much you enjoy her design |

### Common mistakes to avoid

- **Pulling the weapon banner first.** Secure the character before the weapon.
- **Building Crit on Shorekeeper.** Her own damage is irrelevant; HP% and Energy Regen are what matter.
- **Ignoring Energy Regen.** Her buffs live and die by burst uptime.
- **Waiting until the last hours of the banner.** Server maintenance and last-minute decisions cause real regrets.
- **Pulling without a full pity saved.** Hope is not a strategy.

If you want a general rule: pull for Shorekeeper if your account lacks a dedicated Crit buffer, and skip if you already have strong sustain and a clear next target. A Shorekeeper rerun will come around again, but so will everything else — plan for the account you actually have.

## FAQ

**When is the next Shorekeeper rerun?**
Kuro Games has not published a permanent rerun schedule. Returns are announced patch by patch through official channels, so the safest approach is to follow the official announcements and keep a pity buffer ready rather than trusting rumor dates.

**Is Shorekeeper still worth pulling in 2026?**
For most accounts, yes. Team-wide Crit Rate and Crit DMG buffs age extremely well because they scale with whatever DPS you happen to be using. A Shorekeeper rerun is one of the few banners that improves your entire roster rather than a single team.

**How many pulls should I save for a Shorekeeper rerun?**
Plan for a worst-case scenario rather than an average one. Saving enough for a full pity — and ideally a guaranteed featured 5-star — means you will not be caught short if you lose the 50/50.

**Does my pity carry over into a Shorekeeper rerun?**
Yes. Pity on the limited character banner carries across banners of the same type, so pulls you have already made toward a previous 5-star still count. Weapon banner pity is tracked separately, so budget for it independently.
